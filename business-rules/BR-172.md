## Rule

`Dockerfile` defines stages `build`, `deps`, `runtime` and `smoke`, in that order. `build` and `deps` start `FROM --platform=$BUILDPLATFORM` and `runtime` starts without `--platform`; `smoke` is `FROM runtime`. Every external `FROM` names `node:26.13.0-bookworm` (`build`, `deps`) or `node:26.13.0-bookworm-slim` (`runtime`) with an `@sha256:<64 hex>` digest. Each of `build` and `runtime` runs a check that `node --version` equals `v26.13.0`.

## Rationale

Native build stages keep `npm ci` and `tsc` out of emulation; digest pins make the base reproducible and reviewable. Source: [F-013 v1](../features/F-013-http-image.md).
