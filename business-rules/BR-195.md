## Rule

At most 8 upstream requests are in flight per process; further requests wait first-in first-out in a queue of at most 64, the wait counting toward the BR-193 deadline; a request arriving at a full queue is refused with the unbranded failure of BR-196. A download keeps its slot while its body reader is open and releases it, destroying the socket, at end of stream, `close`, a failed `read`, or 5,000 ms with no pending `read`. Verified by: AC-06.

## Rationale

Bounded admission keeps one process from opening unbounded upstream connections and keeps waiting work inside the deadline. Source: [F-014 v1](../features/F-014-production-kdrive-adapter.md).

## Examples

- **Given** 8 requests in flight and 64 waiting
  **When** a 73rd request starts
  **Then** it is refused `READ_UNAVAILABLE` without a request.
- **Given** 8 open body readers and one waiter
  **When** one reader is closed
  **Then** the waiter starts and the reader's socket is destroyed.
