## Rule

A retry starts only for a branded pre-body normalized read failure with status429 or500–599, and never exceeds three attempts for that logical call or the invocation's shared limits.

## Rationale

One bounded retry policy avoids implicit amplification and arbitrary error-object access. Source: [F-007 v1](../features/F-007-bounded-downloads.md).

## Examples

- **Given** a branded pre-body429 fails twice then succeeds.
  **When** a logical injected read is invoked.
  **Then** three starts occur at0/100/300ms inside shared limits.
- **Given** a fifth list attempt fails retryably.
  **When** another attempt would be needed.
  **Then** LIMIT_EXCEEDED prevents a sixth list start.
