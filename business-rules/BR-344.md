# BR-344 — Privacy scan fails closed on a missing or invalid secret

## Rule

`make privacy-scan` exits 2 with a fixed message and no scan when `PRIVACY_DENY_PATTERNS` is unset, empty or yields zero patterns, and when any pattern fails to compile; the latter prints only `privacy-scan: pattern <n> is not a valid regex`.

## Rationale

A missing secret (fork, Dependabot, deleted secret) or an unparseable pattern must never turn the scan into a silent pass (T4). Source: [F-023 v1](../features/F-023-hosted-ci-privacy-scan.md).

## Examples

- **Given** `PRIVACY_DENY_PATTERNS` unset, empty or comments only
  **When** the scan runs
  **Then** it exits 2 with `privacy-scan: no pattern is configured` on stderr and nothing on stdout
- **Given** a secret whose line 2 is an invalid regex
  **When** the scan runs
  **Then** it exits 2 with `privacy-scan: pattern 2 is not a valid regex` only
