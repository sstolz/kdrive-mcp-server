## Rule

A process that stops between a resolved intent append and its outcome append leaves that complete `intent` record as the last record with its `op`, and no other record is written for that `op` by a later process.

## Rationale

An orphan intent is the durable evidence of a crash between intent and outcome. Source: [F-016 v1](../features/F-016-mutation-audit-gate.md).

## Examples

- **Given** a gate process killed with SIGKILL inside its mutator
  **When** a second process completes one run on the same file
  **Then** the first `op` has only its intact intent line
- **Given** the second run
  **When** it completes
  **Then** its intent and outcome follow the orphan intent
