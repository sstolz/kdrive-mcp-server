## Rule

A serialized record escapes every control character (U+0000– U+001F, U+007F) and U+2028/U+2029, escapes every identity code point outside U+0020–U+007E as `\uXXXX` (C1 controls and bidi overrides included), and is at most 8,192 bytes including its LF. A gate run is refused at admission as `failed` with code `AUDIT_UNAVAILABLE`, without latching the sink, when its identity `iss` exceeds 4,096 UTF-8 bytes, its `sub` exceeds 1,024 UTF-8 bytes, its identity strings are empty or not well-formed Unicode, or its largest possible record (the `outcome` record with every optional member at its widest value) serializes to more than 8,192 bytes. An append of a record over 8,192 bytes rejects without I/O and without latching.

## Rationale

One bounded printable line per record prevents log injection and partial interleaving (D-16-3, D-16-7). Source: [F-016 v1](../features/F-016-mutation-audit-gate.md).

## Examples

- **Given** an OAuth `sub` containing LF, U+0085 and U+202E
  **When** a run is recorded
  **Then** each record is one printable-ASCII line with those characters escaped
- **Given** a `sub` of 1,025 bytes
  **When** a run starts
  **Then** it is refused `AUDIT_UNAVAILABLE` with zero records and zero policy calls
