# BR-058 — Ordinary common folders

## Rule

Common/team folders on configured non-external drives use the ordinary configured-tree rules regardless of shared flags or visibility metadata.

The detailed frozen evidence, capability, cache and effect contracts in
[F-004 v1](../features/F-004-received-share-policy.md) are normative.

## Rationale

Team/private visibility must not add an unrequested permission filter.
