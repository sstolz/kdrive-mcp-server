# BR-284 — Upload mutators send only `conflict=error` or `conflict=version`

## Rule

The `upload-new` mutator sends `conflict=error` and the `replace` mutator sends `conflict=version`; no other `conflict` value is ever sent; `directory_id` is the operation's destination ID, `file_name` its `name` and `total_size` the payload byte count; each sends exactly one request per attempt and no other request (BR-271).

## Rationale

Owner decision D-07 option A maps the overwrite flag to the two measured conflict modes (D-18-1). Source: [F-018 v1](../features/F-018-upload.md).

## Examples

- **Given** an `upload-new` of 16 bytes into folder 40
  **When** the mutator runs
  **Then** one `POST /3/drive/101/upload?directory_id=40&file_name=…&total_size=16&conflict=error`
- **Given** a `replace` of file 41 in folder 40
  **When** the mutator runs
  **Then** one `POST` with `conflict=version`
