## Rule

In `examples/**` and `docs/deployment.md`: every scanned host (the host of each `scheme://…` URL, the domain after `@` in each email address, and each argument of a Traefik `Host(…)` rule) is `example.com`, `example.org`, `example.net`, a subdomain of them, or `localhost`; the only IP addresses are `0.0.0.0` and `127.0.0.1`; after every URL is removed, no path starting with `/home/`, `/Users/`, `/root/`, `/srv/`, `/opt/`, `/var/`, `/etc/`, `/mnt/`, `/media/` or `/tmp/`, and no Windows drive path, appears; installation-specific Compose values are `${NAME:?…}` variables.

## Rationale

Examples never leak a real hostname or installation detail. Source: [F-013 v1](../features/F-013-http-image.md).
