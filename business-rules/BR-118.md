## Rule

**v2 (active until v3 acceptance; unchanged)**

Cancellation, deadline and close terminate owned extraction work before the invocation slot is released and suppress late publication.

v1 (accepted; superseded by v2): the same statement, with the example "a pending read and a concurrent info call → `SERVER_BUSY`".

**v3 (F-040 v1, #47 lot D, pending acceptance; v2 stays active until then)** —
The v2 lifecycle holds except when owned worker termination rejects before
exit or remains unresolved at the invocation deadline: transfer its extraction
permit and identity charge to a detached exit watcher, settle the invocation
and release its session slot no later than that original deadline, and retain
the permit until confirmed worker exit. A live termination rejection returns
`CONTENT_UNAVAILABLE` only after the unchanged BR-109 final authorization;
denial returns `ACCESS_DENIED`, and stalled authorization or termination at
the deadline returns `REQUEST_TIMEOUT` unless an earlier cancel/close preserves
`ACCESS_DENIED`. No fresh policy call starts after abort/deadline/close. Close
suppresses wire publication; late exit/authorization/termination events cannot
publish a second result or release twice. Ordinary confirmed exit still
precedes session-slot release. BR-426 defines fixed detach/release diagnostics;
BR-424 refuses extraction for an identity with a detached charged permit.

## Rationale

Cancelling an unresponsive CPU task must not leak a slot or publish late data. Source: [F-009 v1](../features/F-009-bounded-content-reading.md).

Pending v3 bounds invocation settlement without recycling a still-running
worker's permit; memory accounting and final authorization remain intact.
Source: [F-040 frozen v1](../features/F-040-process-wide-extraction-admission.md),
D-4, AC-07 and AC-08. The preceding v2 rule and its acceptance remain unchanged.

## Examples

- **Given** extractTimeoutMs1 and an owned worker.
  **When** its deadline fires.
  **Then** REQUEST_TIMEOUT follows owned worker termination.
- **Given** a pending read and a concurrent info call.
  **When** the second call starts.
  **Then** the info call waits and runs after the read settles (owned worker terminated first), and later slot reuse works (v2; v1: SERVER_BUSY is returned).
- **Given** a pending read and 4 waiting calls (v2).
  **When** a further call starts.
  **Then** SERVER_BUSY is returned and the read's worker lifecycle is unaffected.

Pending v3 examples (the preceding examples describe active v2):

- **Given** A starts at t=0 and its worker keeps running
  **When** termination rejects at t=1,000 and final authorization succeeds immediately
  **Then** `CONTENT_UNAVAILABLE` settles at t=1,000 and the session slot
  releases, while the detached watcher retains the permit until confirmed
  exit at t=10,002; no second result occurs. Maps to AC-08 `termination-rejection`.
- **Given** the same worker with no earlier cancel/close
  **When** termination is still pending at t=10,000
  **Then** `REQUEST_TIMEOUT` settles at the deadline and the slot releases;
  the permit stays charged until confirmed exit. Maps to AC-08
  `termination-pending-at-deadline`.
- **Given** that worker and cancel/close recorded at t=1,000
  **When** termination/exit remain pending at t=10,000
  **Then** `ACCESS_DENIED` settles by t=10,000, the session slot releases,
  close publishes no wire result, and the detached permit stays charged.
  Maps to AC-08 `cancel-pending-at-deadline` / `close-pending-at-deadline`.
- **Given** termination rejection at t=1,000
  **When** final authorization denies at t=1,001 or stalls past t=10,000
  **Then** settlement is respectively `ACCESS_DENIED` at t=1,001 or
  `REQUEST_TIMEOUT` at t=10,000; the slot releases and the permit remains
  charged until exit, with no late publication. Maps to AC-08
  `termination-rejection-final-denial` / `termination-rejection-final-auth-deadline`.
