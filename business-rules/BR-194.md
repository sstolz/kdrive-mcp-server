## Rule

The adapter counts body bytes before parsing or keeping them and refuses a JSON success body above 4 MiB, an error or redirect body above 8 KiB, and any `Content-Encoding` other than absent or `identity`. Verified by: AC-05.

## Rationale

Unbounded or compressed bodies would let the provider or a man-in-the-middle exhaust memory before any check runs. Source: [F-014 v1](../features/F-014-production-kdrive-adapter.md).

## Examples

- **Given** a JSON success body of exactly 4,194,304 bytes
  **When** it is received
  **Then** it is accepted.
- **Given** a JSON success body of 4,194,305 bytes
  **When** it is received
  **Then** it is refused unbranded before parsing.
