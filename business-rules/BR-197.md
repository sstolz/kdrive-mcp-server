## Rule

Every port call issues its own fresh upstream requests: the adapter keeps no response, ancestry, role, capability or ETag cache between port calls.

## Rationale

F-003 forbids hidden evidence reuse; every authorization decision must rest on fresh provider evidence. Source: [F-014 v1](../features/F-014-production-kdrive-adapter.md).

## Examples

- **Given** two consecutive `inspect` calls with a rename in between
  **When** both run
  **Then** the second result has the new name and two metadata requests were made.
- **Given** two received `inspect` calls with the role changed to `admin` in between
  **When** both run
  **Then** the second is `incomplete` and two init requests were made.
