## Rule

A refresh token is 32 random bytes, stored only as its SHA-256 with its family, client ID, identity, resource, issue time and state (current or rotated). A family's absolute expiry is its creation + 30 days; a token's inactivity expiry is its issue + 7 days; a token is usable while now is before both. Each successful refresh marks the presented token as rotated and issues a new token in the same family; rotation never extends the family's absolute expiry. Rotated tokens stay recorded until the family ends so that reuse is detected.

Version 2 (F-019 v1, #103; accepted on main `68209fe`, 2026-11-11): a refresh token is `<family id>.<secret>`: the family ID is 16 random bytes and the secret 32 random bytes, both base64url without padding (66 characters). A family stores the client ID, identity, resource, creation time and only the SHA-256 of its current secret (input in Contract details) with its issue time. Expiries and rotation are unchanged; rotated secrets are not kept.

## Rationale

Owner decision D-05: 30-day absolute and 7-day inactivity refresh lifetimes with rotation. Source: [F-010 v1](../features/F-010-oauth-proxy.md), AC-20, AC-21.

## Examples

- **Given** a family created at t refreshed every 6 days
  **When** a token is presented at t + 30 days
  **Then** 400 `invalid_grant`.
- **Given** a token issued at u and unused
  **When** it is presented at u + 7 days
  **Then** 400 `invalid_grant`.
