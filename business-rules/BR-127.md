## Rule

The registration handler takes the raw body text; a body over 16,384 UTF-8 bytes, not a JSON object, or with an invalid member yields 400 `{"error":"invalid_client_metadata"}`. `redirect_uris` is required: a nonempty array of at most 10 distinct strings, each matching an entry of `oauthAllowedRedirectUris` under BR-126; otherwise 400 `{"error":"invalid_redirect_uri"}`. `token_endpoint_auth_method` is `none`, `client_secret_basic` (default when absent, RFC 7591) or `client_secret_post`; `grant_types` is absent (meaning both) or a nonempty subset of `authorization_code` and `refresh_token` that contains `authorization_code`; `response_types` is absent or `["code"]`; `client_name` is absent or a string of 1–200 characters without control characters. Other members are ignored and never stored or echoed. Success returns 201 with `Cache-Control: no-store` and `client_id` (32 random bytes), `client_id_issued_at`, the stored `redirect_uris`, `grant_types`, `response_types` `["code"]`, `token_endpoint_auth_method`, `client_name` when given, and for a secret method a `client_secret` (32 random bytes) with `client_secret_expires_at` `0`; only the SHA-256 of the secret is stored.

## Rationale

Open dynamic registration is limited to the owner's redirect allowlist so that registered clients cannot receive codes elsewhere. Source: [F-010 v1](../features/F-010-oauth-proxy.md), AC-04, AC-05.

## Examples

- **Given** an allowlist with the claude.ai callback
  **When** a client registers it without a method
  **Then** 201 with method `client_secret_basic` and a 43-character secret.
- **Given** the same allowlist
  **When** a client registers `https://evil.test/cb`
  **Then** 400 `invalid_redirect_uri` and no client is stored.
