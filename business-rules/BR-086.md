## Rule

A resource success contains one complete raw binary blob and never a truncated blob, extracted text or sliced MCP image.

## Rationale

A binary resource is useful only if its complete blob has a truthful representation. Source: [F-007 v1](../features/F-007-bounded-downloads.md).

## Examples

- **Given** a three-byte image or other binary fits all output/read bounds.
  **When** its canonical resource is read.
  **Then** one complete application/octet-stream blob decodes to all three bytes.
- **Given** the whole representation exceeds the character budget by one.
  **When** the resource is read.
  **Then** LIMIT_EXCEEDED occurs before opening, with no partial blob.
