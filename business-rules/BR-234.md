## Rule

Each audit record is one JSON object followed by one LF, whose members are exactly those of the record schema below for its `event`.

## Rationale

A fixed, closed schema keeps records machine-checkable and free of accidental content (SPEC §9). Source: [F-016 v1](../features/F-016-mutation-audit-gate.md).

## Examples

- **Given** an intent for `kdrive_rename` on drive 101, target 42
  **When** it is serialized
  **Then** the line is the spec example ending in one LF
- **Given** a policy-refused move from drive 101 to drive 102
  **When** the gate records the denial
  **Then** the `denied` record carries `drive:101` then `destinationDrive:102`
