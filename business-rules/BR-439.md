## Rule

A mutation request closing before any `response` event settles immediately
under BR-267 v1 as unbranded `MUTATION_UNKNOWN` with its socket destroyed and
limiter slot released, without replay or waiting for abort.

## Rationale

A close without a normal response, including a 101 upgrade, cannot retain a
concurrency slot until caller cancellation. A final non-1xx response retains
BR-268 v3's status and body handling.
Source: [F-051 v1](../features/F-051-transport-edge-cases.md), AC-03a–AC-03b.

## Examples

- **Given** seven held mutations, an eighth receiving 101 with both upgrade
  headers and no normal response event, and a ninth queued mutation
  **When** the eighth request closes
  **Then** it rejects as `MUTATION_UNKNOWN`, destroys its socket and releases
  its slot so the ninth completes while the seven remain held; its signal is
  never aborted and its single attempt is not replayed. Maps to
  `TestBR439_v1_101_close_releases_slot_without_abort`.
- **Given** the same nine-mutation arrangement and zero response bytes for
  the eighth request
  **When** its socket closes
  **Then** the same immediate unknown settlement, destruction and slot release
  occur without abort or replay. Maps to
  `TestBR439_v1_zero_byte_close_releases_slot_without_abort`.

## Verification

`TestBR439_v1_101_close_releases_slot_without_abort` and
`TestBR439_v1_zero_byte_close_releases_slot_without_abort` in
`tests/kdrive-mutation-port.test.ts` (F-051 v1 AC-03a–AC-03b). Both include
gate assertions of `uncertain` / `UPSTREAM_UNCERTAIN`, attempts 1.
