## Rule

Received `list` uses only the unscoped `/3/drive/files/shared_with_me`; received `inspect` uses the metadata route, and received `children` and `collection` use the BR-201 v3/BR-202 v3 routes, all on the request's `driveId` (the share owner's drive) with the BR-203 v3 normalization below. Every received result carries `role` from a fresh `/2/drive/init?with=drives` lookup for that drive and is complete only when that role is `external`. The provider chain is the `parents` members ordered and linked as in BR-199 v2 (exactly one of provider or reverse order links each member's `parent_id` to the previous member's `id`, the item's `parent_id` is the last member's `id` when the chain is nonempty, IDs are unique and never `1`; the first member's `parent_id` is not constrained), or empty when `parents` is absent or an empty array; each member's `type` is `dir`, its `drive_id` when present equals the requested drive, and its `status` member when present is `null` or `"ok"`. A root is an item whose provider `path` is `/Shared/<name>` with its own name; every chain member is dropped, its relative chain is empty, its `anchorId` is its own ID and its recipient path is `/Shared/<name>`. A descendant's provider `path` is `/Shared/c1/…/cn` with n ≥ 2 nonempty components and `cn` equal to its name; its provider chain has at least n−1 members, and its last n−1 members carry exactly the names c1 … c(n−1) in that order. Its anchor is the first of those n−1 members, its relative chain is those n−1 members (the anchor included, as F-004 `parse()` requires; earlier members are dropped), and the recipient path of the i-th relative member is `/Shared/c1/…/ci`. A `path` member of a `parents` entry is never read. Any other shape is `{status:"incomplete"}`.

## Rationale

Recording run `20261109073932` measured received-share ancestry for a share root directly under the owner's root: `parents` members carry no `path`. Other placements remain a hypothesis qualified by AC-25; the adapter accepts only the specified shape and a fresh external role, and refuses everything else. Source: [F-014 v4](../features/F-014-production-kdrive-adapter.md).

## Examples

- **Given** a grandchild with path `/Shared/team/sub/doc` whose provider parents are an owner folder, `team` and `sub`, none carrying `path`
  **When** received `inspect` runs with role `external`
  **Then** its anchor is the `team` member and its parents are `team` (`/Shared/team`) and `sub` (`/Shared/team/sub`).
- **Given** the same grandchild whose provider parents are only `sub`
  **When** received `inspect` runs with role `external`
  **Then** it returns `{status:"incomplete"}` (chain shorter than n−1).
- **Given** the same drive with role `admin`
  **When** received `inspect` runs
  **Then** it returns `{status:"incomplete"}`.
