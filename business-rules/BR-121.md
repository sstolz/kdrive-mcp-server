## Rule

Source envelopes and worker outputs are validated from own data properties and copied into declared projections without invoking hooks.

## Rationale

Own-data projection prevents hooks and untrusted serialization from escaping the fixed schema. Source: [F-009 v1](../features/F-009-bounded-content-reading.md).

## Examples

- **Given** a worker text accessor with a canary hook.
  **When** projection validates it.
  **Then** the accessor is not called and the value refuses.
- **Given** plain bounded worker output.
  **When** projection runs.
  **Then** only declared output keys are copied.
