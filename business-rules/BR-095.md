## Rule

Each capability (PKCE S256, authorization-code grant, dynamic registration, CIMD, JWKS, `none`, `client_secret_basic` and `client_secret_post` token-endpoint authentication) is `yes`, `no` or `unknown` per usable document; the effective value over the usable documents is `yes` for yes+yes or yes+unknown, `unknown` with a listed contradiction for yes+no, `no` for no+no or no+unknown and `unknown` for unknown+unknown; different `jwks_uri` values make JWKS `unknown`, listed; `authorization_response_iss_parameter_supported` is `yes` only for boolean `true`, `no` when `false` or absent, and `no` with a listed anomaly otherwise.

## Rationale

The qualification must not over-read metadata: an absent field is not support, and contradicting documents must not yield a positive capability. Source: [F-008 v1](../features/F-008-oidc-qualification.md), AC-03, AC-04.

## Examples

- **Given** `code_challenge_methods_supported` is `["S256"]` in one usable document and `["plain"]` in the other.
  **When** capabilities are evaluated.
  **Then** PKCE S256 is `unknown` and the contradiction is listed.
- **Given** `code_challenge_methods_supported` is `["S256"]` in one usable document and absent in the other.
  **When** capabilities are evaluated.
  **Then** PKCE S256 is `yes`.
- **Given** `client_id_metadata_document_supported` is the string `"true"`.
  **When** capabilities are evaluated.
  **Then** CIMD is `no`.
