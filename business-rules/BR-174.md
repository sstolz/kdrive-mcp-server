## Rule

The `runtime` stage ends with `USER 1000:1000`. In the runtime image, `find /app \( ! -user 0 -o \( ! -type l -perm /022 \) \) -print` prints nothing.

## Rationale

A non-root, numeric user that orchestrators can enforce, and application files the process cannot modify. Source: [F-013 v1](../features/F-013-http-image.md).

## Examples

- **Given** a `runtime` stage without `USER`
  **When** the static policy runs
  **Then** it fails
- **Given** the built runtime image
  **When** the `find` probe runs
  **Then** it prints nothing
