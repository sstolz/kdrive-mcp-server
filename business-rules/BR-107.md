## Rule

A JWKS key is usable only when its `kty` is `RSA`, `EC` with `crv` `P-256` or `P-384`, or `OKP` with `crv` `Ed25519`, it has no private member (`d`, `p`, `q`, `dp`, `dq`, `qi`, `k`), its `use` is absent or `sig`, its `key_ops` is absent or contains `verify`, and its `alg` is absent or in BR-098's list; a JWKS containing any private member is unusable as a whole.

## Rationale

Only verification keys of accepted algorithms may authenticate tokens, and a provider leaking private keys must not be trusted. Source: [F-008 v1](../features/F-008-oidc-qualification.md), AC-04.

  **Then** JWKS is `no`.
- **Given** a JWKS with a valid key and an RSA key carrying `p`.
  **When** JWKS is evaluated.
  **Then** JWKS is `no`.
