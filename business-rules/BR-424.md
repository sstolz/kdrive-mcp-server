# BR-424 — Bounded extraction waiting by oldest eligible identity

## Rule

Contended extraction admission retains at most four live waiters,
serves the oldest live waiter whose identity holds no active permit without
newcomers overtaking an older eligible waiter, and
when no immediate eligible grant is possible and the queue is full,
immediately refuses a further live caller with exactly
`{isError:true,content:[{type:"text",text:"LIMIT_EXCEEDED"}]}` without an
automatic retry, source work or an admission-specific diagnostic/event.

## Rationale

A bounded queue limits retained work independently of the HTTP session cap.
The error uses the existing fixed tool-result shape and reveals no target,
content, dependency error, queue count or position. `SERVER_BUSY` remains the
existing per-session queue result with its separate retry guidance. Waiting
and handoff spend no upstream-operation budget. Source:
[F-040 v1](../features/F-040-process-wide-extraction-admission.md),
contract details and D-1/D-3. Skipped ineligible waiters keep their original
position; detached worker permits still charge their identity. A second call
from an active identity waits even with spare global capacity. Queue places
are shared, without per-identity reservations. README and the actual
`kdrive_read` tool description must explain that `LIMIT_EXCEEDED` may be
transient for queue saturation, that the server does not automatically retry,
and that a later client invocation needs fresh admission and authorization.
Permanent size limits and worker error/exit keep the same existing code.

## Examples

Unless explicitly shared, all example controllers have distinct identities.

- **E-01 — Given** two active pipelines and three live extraction waiters
  **When** a fourth waiter arrives
  **Then** it waits without starting content and follows the preceding three.
  Maps to `TestBR424_v1_AC02_four_waiters_are_FIFO_without_barging`,
  fourth enqueue.
- **E-02 — Given** two active pipelines and four live extraction waiters
  **When** the seventh caller (fifth would-be waiter) arrives
  **Then** it returns the exact `LIMIT_EXCEEDED` result immediately and never
  starts or retries later, even when a permit becomes available. Maps to
  `TestBR424_v1_AC03_seventh_caller_fifth_waiter_refuses_LIMIT_EXCEEDED_without_retry`.
- **E-03 — Given** a released permit handed to the oldest eligible of four waiters
  **When** a new caller arrives during the handoff
  **Then** it joins behind the three remaining waiters and never overtakes
  them. Maps to `TestBR424_v1_AC02_four_waiters_are_FIFO_without_barging`,
  newcomer case.
- **E-04 — Given** full capacity and canary target/source/dependency values
  **When** another extraction is refused
  **Then** its only admission signal is the fixed tool error, with no canary,
  queue/retry field, new diagnostic or audit event. Maps to
  `TestBR424_v1_AC09_admission_errors_are_fixed_and_silent`, full-queue row.

- **E-05 — Given** A1 and B1 active, with A2, C1, B2, D1 queued in order
  **When** B1 releases
  **Then** C1 starts; A2 keeps its age but cannot start while A1 holds a
  permit. After C1 releases, B2 starts before D1; after A1 releases, A2
  starts before D1. Maps to
  `TestBR423_v1_AC11_identity_cap_and_oldest_eligible_waiter`,
  `oldest-eligible` and `no-barging` rows (also asserting BR-424 v1).
