# BR-282 — `kdrive_upload` annotations

## Rule

`kdrive_upload` carries `readOnlyHint:false`, `destructiveHint:true`, `idempotentHint:false` and `openWorldHint:true`.

## Rationale

Annotations are per tool and `overwrite:true` replaces content (D-18-5). Source: [F-018 v1](../features/F-018-upload.md).

## Examples

- **Given** mode `write`
  **When** tools are listed
  **Then** `kdrive_upload` has `destructiveHint:true`
- **Given** mode `read`
  **When** tools are listed
  **Then** `kdrive_upload` is absent
