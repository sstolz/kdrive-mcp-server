## Rule

For an authorized mutation, the gate appends the `intent` record and awaits its resolution before the first upstream attempt starts, and appends the `outcome` record only after the final classification; both carry the same `op`, 32 lowercase hexadecimal characters from 16 bytes of the injected random source.

## Rationale

The intent must exist before any upstream effect, and both records must correlate. Source: [F-016 v1](../features/F-016-mutation-audit-gate.md).

## Examples

- **Given** a mutator that inspects the log
  **When** it is called
  **Then** the intent record is already written
- **Given** random bytes 00…0f
  **When** a run succeeds
  **Then** intent and outcome carry `op` `000102030405060708090a0b0c0d0e0f`
