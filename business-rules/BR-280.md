# BR-280 — Upload content decoding and bound

## Rule

The content bytes are the UTF-8 encoding of `text`, or the decoding of `base64`; `text` with an unpaired surrogate, and `base64` that is not canonical RFC 4648 §4, return `INVALID_ARGUMENTS`; content of more than 524,288 bytes returns `LIMIT_EXCEEDED`; both refusals make zero `mutate` calls, zero records and zero upstream requests; 0 bytes is valid. Checks apply in the order schema, encoding, byte bound, `mutate`, and the first failure is returned.

## Rationale

A fixed bound below the measured 1 MiB fits a base64 stdio frame; a strict order makes refusals predictable (D-18-3). Source: [F-018 v1](../features/F-018-upload.md).

## Examples

- **Given** `base64` `"QQ=="`
  **When** `kdrive_upload` is called
  **Then** a 1-byte payload `A`
- **Given** `base64` `"QR=="` (nonzero pad bits)
  **When** `kdrive_upload` is called
  **Then** `INVALID_ARGUMENTS`
- **Given** `text` of 524,288 ASCII characters
  **When** `kdrive_upload` is called
  **Then** one `mutate` call
- **Given** `text` of 524,289 ASCII characters
  **When** `kdrive_upload` is called
  **Then** `LIMIT_EXCEEDED` and zero `mutate` calls
