## Rule

Each content attempt and each publication uses the existing live read policy for the exact owner drive and file; denial starts no protected content call and publishes no bytes.

## Rationale

Exact-file policy and received-share freshness protect content as they protect metadata. Source: [F-007 v1](../features/F-007-bounded-downloads.md).

## Examples

- **Given** drive7 file3 is a permitted live file.
  **When** the raw tool and canonical resource read it.
  **Then** both succeed through fresh read authorization.
- **Given** the same file becomes denied before final authorization.
  **When** publication is attempted.
  **Then** no bytes are returned.
