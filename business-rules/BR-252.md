## Rule

When the outcome append rejects after an upstream attempt was made, the gate returns the attempt's classification (`succeeded`, `failed` or `uncertain`, with its code and value) unchanged plus `audit: "outcome_unrecorded"`, latches the sink, writes the fixed diagnostic `Audit outcome could not be recorded; further writes are refused until restart.` to stderr once, and neither reports `AUDIT_UNAVAILABLE`, nor replays, nor compensates the write.

## Rationale

A performed write must never be reported as prevented (D-16-1, resolves D-06). Source: [F-016 v1](../features/F-016-mutation-audit-gate.md).

## Examples

- **Given** an outcome append failing after a 200
  **When** the run ends
  **Then** the result is `succeeded` with `audit:"outcome_unrecorded"` and one fixed diagnostic
- **Given** the same failure
  **When** a next run starts
  **Then** it is refused `AUDIT_UNAVAILABLE` at admission
