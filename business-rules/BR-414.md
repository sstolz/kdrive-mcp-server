# BR-414 — Stream decoding

## Rule

Only the streams BR-412, BR-413, BR-416, BR-417, BR-419 and BR-420 need are decoded; any other stream is framed by its `/Length` and never decoded, whatever its filter. A needed stream has no filter, or a `/Filter` that is a name or an array of one or two names: `/FlateDecode`, `/ASCII85Decode` or `/ASCIIHexDecode` alone, or `/ASCII85Decode` or `/ASCIIHexDecode` followed by `/FlateDecode`; any other filter, order or count refuses as `UNSUPPORTED_FORMAT`. `/DecodeParms` is absent, `null`, a dictionary (with one filter) or an array as long as the filter array whose entries are dictionaries or `null`; only the entry of `/FlateDecode` may be a dictionary, and a dictionary for an ASCII filter refuses as `UNSUPPORTED_FORMAT`. The `/FlateDecode` parameters have `/Predictor` 1 or 10–15, `/Colors` 1–4, `/BitsPerComponent` 8 and `/Columns` 1–65,536; PNG rows are undone with each row's own tag and the predicted data is a whole number of rows. `/ASCIIHexDecode` reads pairs of hexadecimal digits, skipping white space, up to `>` (a final single digit is followed by 0); `/ASCII85Decode` reads groups of five characters `!`–`u`, `z` for four zero bytes at the start of a group, skipping white space, up to `~>`, a final group of 2–4 characters giving 1–3 bytes. Under either filter, a byte other than those it reads (a hexadecimal digit; `!`–`u`, or `z` at the start of a group), white space and its end marker, an ASCII85 group above 2^32 − 1 or final group of one character, or data that ends without its end marker (`>` for `/ASCIIHexDecode`, `~>` for `/ASCII85Decode`) refuses as `CONTENT_UNAVAILABLE`, and bytes after the end marker are ignored. Each needed stream is decoded once per invocation, and the output of each filter of its chain, the ASCII stage before `/FlateDecode` included, counts against B with the F-009 inflate rules (`maxOutputLength` set to the remaining B, `rejectGarbageAfterEnd`); an ASCII stage whose output passes the remaining B refuses as `LIMIT_EXCEEDED` at that byte. Content and Form streams also count their decoded length again at each further use (BR-416, BR-419). Two exceptions apply. A Flate inline image (BR-416) is inflated from the rest of its content stream with `maxOutputLength` set to the smaller of its computed size and the remaining B and without `rejectGarbageAfterEnd`, since the content after its data is not garbage; its data length is the number of input bytes the inflate consumed up to the end of the zlib stream. A Type 1 program (BR-420) has no filter or `/FlateDecode` alone, without `/DecodeParms`; another filter or any `/DecodeParms` refuses as `UNSUPPORTED_FORMAT`, and a `/Length1` over the remaining B refuses as `LIMIT_EXCEEDED` before inflating. Node offers no synchronous inflate that stops early (`inflateSync` with `maxOutputLength` throws and returns no output), so a Flate program is inflated with the asynchronous `zlib.createInflate` with `chunkSize` 16,384: output chunks are kept until `/Length1` bytes are held, then the stream is destroyed and reads no further input. Every byte it produced, at most `/Length1` + 16,383, counts against B, and output passing the remaining B refuses as `LIMIT_EXCEEDED`; a program whose output (or unfiltered data) ends before `/Length1` bytes refuses as `CONTENT_UNAVAILABLE`.

## Rationale

Images and font programs use filters text never needs; not decoding them removes their decoders and their budget. Flate with PNG predictors covers the cross-reference and content streams of common generators (F-034 S04, S05), and ReportLab writes `[/ASCII85Decode /FlateDecode]` on every stream (F-034 S30). Node has no synchronous inflate that stops early, so the Type 1 program inflate is asynchronous and its overshoot of one chunk is counted. Source: [F-034 v1](../features/F-034-pdf-extraction-profile.md).

## Examples

- **Given** Flate content with predictor 12 whose inflated bytes bring the decoded total to exactly B
  **When** page 1 is read
  **Then** its text
- **Given** the same content bringing the decoded total to B + 1 bytes
  **When** page 1 is read
  **Then** `LIMIT_EXCEEDED` before PDF.js
- **Given** a predictor with `/Columns` 65,536 and `/Colors` 4
  **When** page 1 is read
  **Then** its text
- **Given** a predictor with `/Columns` 65,537
  **When** page 1 is read
  **Then** `LIMIT_EXCEEDED` before PDF.js
- **Given** a page that also draws a `DCTDecode` image whose data is not a JPEG
  **When** page 1 is read
  **Then** its text, the image never decoded
- **Given** a Type 1 `FontFile` whose inflated output is shorter than its `/Length1`
  **When** page 1 is read
  **Then** `CONTENT_UNAVAILABLE` before PDF.js, when a shown code needs its built-in encoding
- **Given** content with `/Filter [/ASCII85Decode /FlateDecode]`
  **When** page 1 is read
  **Then** its text
- **Given** content with `/Filter [/FlateDecode /ASCII85Decode]`
  **When** page 1 is read
  **Then** `UNSUPPORTED_FORMAT` before PDF.js
- **Given** a Type 1 `FontFile` with `/Length1` 1,000 whose Flate data inflates to 2^30 bytes, and a shown code that needs its built-in encoding
  **When** page 1 is read
  **Then** its text, with at most 1,000 + 16,383 bytes inflated
