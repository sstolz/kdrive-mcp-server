# BR-424 — Bounded extraction waiting by oldest eligible identity

## Rule

**v1 (F-040 v1, #47; accepted on main `67c101a`)**

Contended extraction admission retains at most four live waiters and at most
one live waiter per trusted identity,
serves the oldest live waiter whose identity holds no active permit without
newcomers overtaking an older eligible waiter, and
immediately refuses a caller whose identity holds a detached permit or already
has a live waiter, when every global permit is held by a detached worker,
or who has no immediate eligible grant and finds the queue
full, with exactly
`{isError:true,content:[{type:"text",text:"SERVER_BUSY"}]}` without an
automatic retry, source work or an admission-specific diagnostic/event.

## Rationale

A bounded queue limits retained work independently of the HTTP session cap.
The error uses the existing fixed tool-result shape and reveals no target,
content, dependency error, queue count or position. `SERVER_BUSY` already
means admission refused before upstream work for per-session queue saturation
or wait expiry; later client retry is safe but not guaranteed to succeed. Waiting
and handoff spend no upstream-operation budget. Source:
[F-040 v1](../features/F-040-process-wide-extraction-admission.md),
contract details and D-1/D-3. Skipped ineligible waiters keep their original
position; detached worker permits still charge their identity. A second call
from an active, non-detached identity waits even with spare global capacity;
a third refuses because there is already one waiter. On detach, remove any
already-queued live waiter of that identity with `SERVER_BUSY`; earlier abort
or expiry keeps its liveness code. New calls of that identity refuse immediately
until the detached permit releases. When all permits are detached, new
callers of every identity refuse immediately; existing distinct-identity
waiters retain their own deadlines. Queue places are shared, without reservations.
README, the actual `kdrive_read` description and `docs/mcp-client-compatibility.md`
under `## Parallel tool calls` must explain transient
`SERVER_BUSY` admission refusal (queue saturation, identity waiting limit or
detached permit), no server retry, and fresh admission/authorization for a
later invocation; stuck capacity may persist until exit or process restart.
No recovery time or success on retry is promised; earlier-call completion
alone does not prove detached capacity is reusable. BR-387 is unchanged. Size limits and worker
error/exit retain `LIMIT_EXCEEDED`.

## Examples

Unless explicitly shared, all example controllers have distinct identities.

- **E-01 — Given** two active pipelines and three live extraction waiters
  **When** a fourth waiter arrives
  **Then** it waits without starting content and follows the preceding three.
  Maps to `TestBR424_v1_AC02_four_waiters_are_FIFO_without_barging`,
  fourth enqueue.
- **E-02 — Given** two active pipelines and four live extraction waiters
  **When** the seventh caller (fifth would-be waiter) arrives
  **Then** it returns the exact `SERVER_BUSY` result immediately and never
  starts or retries later, even when a permit becomes available. Maps to
  `TestBR424_v1_AC03_seventh_caller_fifth_waiter_refuses_SERVER_BUSY_without_retry`.
- **E-03 — Given** a released permit handed to the oldest eligible of four waiters
  **When** a new caller arrives during the handoff
  **Then** it joins behind the three remaining waiters and never overtakes
  them. Maps to `TestBR424_v1_AC02_four_waiters_are_FIFO_without_barging`,
  newcomer case.
- **E-04 — Given** full capacity and canary target/source/dependency values
  **When** another extraction is refused
  **Then** its only admission signal is the fixed tool error, with no canary,
  queue/retry field, new diagnostic or audit event. Maps to
  `TestBR424_v1_AC09_admission_errors_are_fixed_and_silent`, full-queue row.

- **E-05 — Given** A1 and B1 active, with A2, C1, B2, D1 queued in order
  **When** B1 releases
  **Then** C1 starts; A2 keeps its age but cannot start while A1 holds a
  permit. After C1 releases, B2 starts before D1; after A1 releases, A2
  starts before D1. Maps to
  `TestBR423_v1_AC11_identity_cap_and_oldest_eligible_waiter`,
  `oldest-eligible` and `no-barging` rows (also asserting BR-424 v1).

- **E-06 — Given** A and B hold permits and C1 waits
  **When** another session C2 requests extraction while three queue places are free
  **Then** C2 immediately returns `SERVER_BUSY` without queueing or source work;
  C1 keeps its age. After cancelling C1, a fresh C3 can enqueue. Maps to
  `TestBR423_v1_AC11_identity_cap_and_oldest_eligible_waiter`,
  `one-waiter-per-identity` row (also asserting BR-424 v1).
- **E-07 — Given** A1 holds a permit and A2 waits in another A session
  **When** A1's worker detaches and A2 is still live
  **Then** A2 is removed with `SERVER_BUSY`; later A calls also immediately
  refuse without queueing or source work, until confirmed exit releases A's
  charge. Maps to `TestBR426_v1_AC08_worker_exit_precedes_permit_reuse`,
  `detach-removes-existing-waiter` and detached rows (also asserting BR-424 v1).
- **E-08 — Given** eight HTTP sessions of A, with A1 active and A2 waiting
  **When** A3–A8 request extraction, then distinct B arrives on the spare permit
  **Then** A3–A8 immediately return `SERVER_BUSY` and B starts without removing
  A2. Four ineligible waiters are impossible with two permits and one waiter
  per identity. Maps to `TestBR423_v1_AC11_identity_cap_and_oldest_eligible_waiter`,
  `eight-http-sessions` row.

- **E-09 — Given** A and B hold detached permits, C is already queued
  **When** a new distinct identity D requests extraction before either exits
  **Then** D immediately receives `SERVER_BUSY`, with no queue entry or
  source work. Skip B's t=10,001 release in this row; no permit is recycled
  before exit. Maps to AC-08 `two-stuck-workers`.
