## Rule

Cancellation, deadline and close prevent new attempts and late publication, settle owned waits and close any opened body.

## Rationale

Abandoned work cannot retain the active slot, leak readers or publish after close. Source: [F-007 v1](../features/F-007-bounded-downloads.md).

## Examples

- **Given** an opened body has a pending read.
  **When** the client cancels the invocation.
  **Then** the reader closes once and no later result publishes.
- **Given** a retry wait or body never settles.
  **When** the10000ms deadline expires.
  **Then** REQUEST_TIMEOUT settles the invocation and no new call starts.
