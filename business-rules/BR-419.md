# BR-419 — Form XObject inlining

## Rule

A `Do` whose name the resource scope's `/XObject` maps to a stream with `/Subtype /Form` (any `/Group`, `/Ref`, `/OC`, `PTEX.*` or other key ignored) is inlined: the canonical content receives `q`, then `cm` with the Form's `/Matrix` when it is present and not the identity, then the Form's projected tokens, then `Q`; the wrapping `q` counts toward the 32-level nesting of BR-416. A `/Matrix` that is not an array of six numbers passing the F-009 `cm` operand check (each finite, with absolute value at most 1,000,000) refuses as `UNSUPPORTED_FORMAT`, as a failing `cm` does. The Form's content is its decoded stream, decoded and tokenized once per invocation and checked with BR-416; every execution after the stream's first use adds its decoded length to the bytes counted against B (BR-414), at the `Do` and before any of its tokens is emitted, and the execution that passes the remaining B refuses as `LIMIT_EXCEEDED`, so dropped operators in a Form are paid for at every execution. The content is checked starting outside `BT` with the caller's graphics state and font; content that ends inside `BT`/`ET` refuses as `CONTENT_UNAVAILABLE`. Its resource scope is its own `/Resources`, or, when it has none, the resource scope of the content that invoked it; its `Tf`, `gs` and `Do` names resolve in that scope, and fonts it selects take the canonical names of BR-415. The projection of a Form is built once per pair of Form and resource scope and reused for later executions with that scope, its nested executions counted again each time; at each execution, its text-show strings are counted (BR-418) and their codes collected (BR-417, BR-420) with the font in effect at that point of the canonical stream, so a Form that shows text with its caller's font adds its codes to that font, once for each font it is drawn with. An inlined Form whose projected tokens, nested Forms included, hold no text-show operator (`Tj`, `TJ`, `'`, `"`) emits nothing; one that shows text before its own first `Tf` needs a font set by its caller (BR-416). Bounds per invocation: at most 256 distinct Forms, nesting at most 8 Forms deep, and at most 4,096 Form executions in total (every `Do` of a Form counts, with or without text); a Form reached again on its own `Do` path is a cycle and refuses as `CONTENT_UNAVAILABLE`; an XObject of another subtype than `Form`, `Image` or `PS` refuses as `UNSUPPORTED_FORMAT`.

## Rationale

OCR text layers, overlays, imposition, LaTeX figures and browser output show text through Form XObjects (F-034 S21–S25). Inlining each execution into the page's canonical content keeps PDF.js on one checked stream and makes every execution count toward the static bound; the execution, nesting and distinct-Form ceilings stop exponential nesting before any output is built (F-034 D-4). Source: [F-034 v1](../features/F-034-pdf-extraction-profile.md).

## Examples

- **Given** an OCRmyPDF page whose invisible text layer is a Form
  **When** page 1 is read
  **Then** its text
- **Given** text Forms nested 8 deep
  **When** page 1 is read
  **Then** its text
- **Given** text Forms nested 9 deep
  **When** page 1 is read
  **Then** `LIMIT_EXCEEDED` before PDF.js
- **Given** a Form drawn 4,096 times
  **When** page 1 is read
  **Then** its text
- **Given** a Form drawn 4,097 times
  **When** page 1 is read
  **Then** `LIMIT_EXCEEDED` before PDF.js
- **Given** a Form cycle A→B→A
  **When** page 1 is read
  **Then** `CONTENT_UNAVAILABLE` before PDF.js
- **Given** a Form whose `/Matrix` holds 1,000,001
  **When** page 1 is read
  **Then** `UNSUPPORTED_FORMAT` before PDF.js, as for a `cm` operand over 1,000,000
- **Given** a page holding 30 nested `q` that draws a Form with one `q` of its own
  **When** page 1 is read
  **Then** its text (32 levels in the canonical stream)
- **Given** the same page whose Form holds two `q` of its own
  **When** page 1 is read
  **Then** `UNSUPPORTED_FORMAT` before PDF.js (33 levels)
- **Given** a Form of dropped operators drawn so that the decoded bytes its executions use bring the total to B + 1
  **When** page 1 is read
  **Then** `LIMIT_EXCEEDED` at the `Do` that passes B, before that execution's tokens
