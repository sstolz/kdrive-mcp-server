# BR-385 — An aborted waiting call leaves the queue

## Rule

When the abort signal of a waiting call fires, the call leaves the queue at once and returns `ACCESS_DENIED`, with zero kDrive requests and zero audit records; its wait timer and abort listener are removed, and the calls behind it keep their order and move up.

## Rationale

A cancelled call must not hold a queue place or block later calls; `ACCESS_DENIED` is what an aborted call gets today before any work (F-026 D-4). Source: [F-026 v1](../features/F-026-bounded-invocation-queue.md).

## Examples

- **Given** a running read and waiting calls A, B, C
  **When** B's abort signal fires
  **Then** B returns `ACCESS_DENIED` at once with zero requests, and A then C run in order
- **Given** a waiting `mutate`
  **When** its abort signal fires
  **Then** it returns `ACCESS_DENIED` and no audit record is written
