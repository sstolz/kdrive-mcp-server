# BR-358 — Closed-set step inputs and credentials

## Rule

The release policy validator rejects any key of `release.yml` not listed in BR-355–BR-358, at every level: top-level keys are exactly `name`, `run-name`, `on`, `permissions`, `concurrency`, `env` and `jobs`; job keys only `if`, `runs-on`, `timeout-minutes`, `permissions`, `needs`, `environment`, `outputs` and `steps`; step keys only `name` (no `${{`), `id` (only `release-npm`), `uses`, `with`, `run` and `env`. `with` keys are exactly those of the source specification for checkout, upload and download; `uses` admits only `actions/checkout`, `actions/upload-artifact` and `actions/download-artifact` pinned to a 40-hex SHA; every `run` is exactly one `make <target>` with no `${{`; each step's `env` key set is exactly the set the source specification lists; inputs reach steps only as `RELEASE_VERSION`, `RELEASE_SHA` and `RELEASE_DRY_RUN` (`'true'` on `verify`'s `make release-npm` and `make release-image`); `RELEASE_PHASE` is the literal `verify`, `image` or `npm` per job; `RELEASE_TARBALL_SHA256` is only on `publish-npm`'s `make release-npm` and `read-back`'s `make release-verify`; the only credential is `GITHUB_TOKEN: ${{ github.token }}`, exactly on every `make release-check`, `publish-image`'s `make release-image` and `make release-verify`; the word `secrets` appears nowhere; `IMAGE_PLATFORMS` is exactly `linux/amd64 linux/arm64` on `make image-binfmt` and `make release-image`.

## Rationale

A closed set leaves no unreviewed path for an input to reach a shell, a credential to reach an extra step, or a job to continue past a failure. Source: [F-024 v2](../features/F-024-gated-publication.md).
