## Rule

Content envelopes are validated from own data properties before use and output is copied into the declared scalar/blob projection without invoking dependency getters or serialization hooks.

## Rationale

Only normalized own data can become content metadata or binary output. Source: [F-007 v1](../features/F-007-bounded-downloads.md).

## Examples

- **Given** descriptor/range envelopes contain exactly the declared own data fields.
  **When** they are validated and published.
  **Then** only declared output keys are serialized.
- **Given** an envelope contains a getter or toJSON hook.
  **When** validation runs.
  **Then** it refuses without invoking the hook and closes any own-data body.
