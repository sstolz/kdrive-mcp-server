# BR-431 — Beta candidate ordering

## Rule

A beta candidate has strictly greater SemVer precedence than every published version except 0.0.0-placeholder.0.

The exhaustive predicates and diagnostics are in
[F-024 v4](../features/F-024-gated-publication.md), ticket #91.

## Rationale

Prevent the beta channel moving backwards, using both npm history objects and exact full SemVer precedence; owner decision 2026-11-17, #91.

## Examples

- **Given** published 1.1.0 and 1.2.0-beta.1
  **When** preflight checks candidate 1.3.0-beta.1
  **Then** passes ordering.
- **Given** published 1.1.0 and 1.2.0-beta.1
  **When** preflight checks candidate 1.1.2-beta.1
  **Then** fails ordering.
