## Rule

Every extraction downloads one entire bounded source version through exact F-007 range/body evidence before decoding any source bytes.

## Rationale

A partial container cannot be truthfully decoded or admitted against complete expansion limits. Source: [F-009 v1](../features/F-009-bounded-content-reading.md).

## Examples

- **Given** a3-byte source and maxReadBytes3.
  **When** a read downloads the source.
  **Then** all3 bytes pass exact range evidence before extraction.
- **Given** a4-byte descriptor and maxReadBytes3.
  **When** a read starts.
  **Then** LIMIT_EXCEEDED occurs before open.
