# BR-320 — CI audit job

## Rule

v3 (F-027 v2, #12; accepted on main `75bd9bb`): v2 holds with "exactly three steps" replaced by "exactly four steps: the pinned checkout with `persist-credentials: false`, `run: make setup`, `run: make audit`, `run: make install-check`"; the policy validator admits `make install-check` only in the `audit` job.

v2 (F-023 v1, #1; accepted on main `ad25fa2`; superseded by v3): v1 holds with "`runs-on` the BR-225 self-hosted label array" replaced by "`runs-on` `ubuntu-24.04`" for the `audit` job.

`.github/workflows/ci.yml` has a job `audit` whose canonical form equals the expected job of the CI policy helper, compared like the `image` job: `runs-on` the BR-225 self-hosted label array, `needs: [changes]`, `if` exactly `needs.changes.outputs.heavy != 'false'`, `timeout-minutes: 20`, no job-level `permissions`, `env`, `strategy` or secrets reference, and exactly three steps: the pinned checkout with `persist-credentials: false`, `run: make setup`, `run: make audit`. The policy validator admits `make audit` only in the `audit` job. `audit` is not one of the five build gates (`lint`, `format`, `typecheck`, `test`, `build`): it is not in `sonar` or `sonar-pr` `needs` nor in the Sonar two-run gate check. `ci-result` needs `audit`.

## Rationale

A finding must be seen before merge, not late at release; a separate job keeps Sonar waiting for the five build gates only (D-3, D-4, BR-149 v2). Source: [F-020 v1](../features/F-020-dependency-audit.md).

## Examples

- **Given** the real workflow
  **When** the policy validator runs
  **Then** it accepts the `audit` job equal to `expectedAuditJob`, with `ci-result` needing `audit`
- **Given** `make audit` added to the `lint` job
  **When** the validator runs
  **Then** it is rejected
- **Given** `audit` added to `sonar` `needs`
  **When** the validator runs
  **Then** it is rejected
- **Given** the `audit` job on the label array
  **When** the validator runs
  **Then** it is rejected (v2)
- **Given** the `audit` job without its `make install-check` step, with a flag on it, or with it moved to another job
  **When** the validator runs
  **Then** it is rejected (v3)
