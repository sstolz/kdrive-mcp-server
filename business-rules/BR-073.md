## Rule

A paged tool invocation starts at most five upstream list-type dependency calls, counting authorization discovery and traversal calls.

## Rationale

Proof discovery must consume the same finite request budget as collection data. Source: [F-006 v1](../features/F-006-filtered-pagination.md).

## Examples

- **Given** five completed discovery pages establish membership.
  **When** a scoped list still needs data.
  **Then** empty success defers data without a sixth list start.
- **Given** a fifth discovery page is nonterminal.
  **When** received discovery runs.
  **Then** LIMIT_EXCEEDED returns without a partial snapshot or cursor.
