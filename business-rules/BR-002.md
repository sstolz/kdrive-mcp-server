# BR-002 — Isolated validated drive binding

## Rule

a client binds one positive safe-integer drive ID for its lifetime.
`info(fileId)` validates a positive safe integer and delegates exactly once with
that drive/file pair. Invalid IDs fail before adapter invocation; interleaved
clients never exchange drive IDs.

## Rationale

prevent cross-drive data access at the earliest client boundary.
