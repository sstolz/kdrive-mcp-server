## Rule

A successful range contains exactly the requested identity, inclusive byte bounds, total size, strong ETag and body length, with no ignored range or full-body fallback.

## Rationale

Ignored ranges and inconsistent envelopes can leak excessive bytes or mix representations. Source: [F-007 v1](../features/F-007-bounded-downloads.md).

## Examples

- **Given** range0–1 has total7 and a matching strong ETag.
  **When** the exact206 response supplies two bytes followed by EOF.
  **Then** those two bytes succeed.
- **Given** the response has status200 or supplies an extra byte.
  **When** the range is validated/consumed.
  **Then** CONTENT_UNAVAILABLE occurs and the reader closes without publication.
