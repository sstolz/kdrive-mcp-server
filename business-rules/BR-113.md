## Rule

Text/selected-page output truncates only at code-point boundaries and reports exact progress while counting the entire serialized output budget.

## Rationale

An envelope must stay complete and progress must remain reconstructable under escaping costs. Source: [F-009 v1](../features/F-009-bounded-content-reading.md).

## Examples

- **Given** escaped Unicode text and an output cap equal to full serialized size.
  **When** projection runs.
  **Then** the whole terminal result fits.
- **Given** the same text and a smaller cap that fits a prefix.
  **When** projection runs.
  **Then** a complete code-point prefix has exact next_offset.
