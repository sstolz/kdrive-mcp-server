## Rule

v2 (F-021 v1, #105; accepted on main `e208996`, 2026-11-11): v1 holds, and the service has no `healthcheck` key (the image's `HEALTHCHECK` applies, reading `MCP_HTTP_PORT: "3000"`) and a comment line containing `HEALTHCHECK` and `docs/deployment.md`.

`examples/compose.yaml` defines one service whose image is `${KDRIVE_MCP_IMAGE:?…}`, with `user: "1000:1000"`, `read_only: true`, `cap_drop: [ALL]`, `security_opt: [no-new-privileges:true]`, `init: true`, `restart: on-failure:3`, `stop_signal: SIGTERM`, `stop_grace_period` of at least 10 s, no `ports` mapping, environment `KDRIVE_MCP_TRANSPORT: http`, `OAUTH_MODE: proxy`, `MCP_HTTP_HOST: 0.0.0.0` and `MCP_HTTP_PORT: "3000"`, Traefik labels routing a `Host(...)` rule to port 3000, and every credential provided as a Compose file secret mounted under `/run/secrets/` and referenced only by `KDRIVE_TOKEN_FILE`, `OAUTH_UPSTREAM_CLIENT_SECRET_FILE` and `OAUTH_SIGNING_KEY_FILE`; it sets no `KDRIVE_TOKEN` or `OAUTH_UPSTREAM_CLIENT_SECRET` value and no `command`. Its service environment, with every `${NAME:?…}` replaced by an RFC 2606 value and the `*_FILE` paths by temporary synthetic files, is accepted by F-002 `loadConfiguration` with transport `http` and OAuth mode `proxy`.

## Rationale

A safe, generic template that stays valid when the HTTP runtime lands. Source: [F-013 v1](../features/F-013-http-image.md).
