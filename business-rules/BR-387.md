# BR-387 — Parallel calls and SERVER_BUSY are documented

## Rule

README "Key limits" and the section `## Parallel tool calls` of `docs/mcp-client-compatibility.md` state that one call per session runs at a time, that up to 4 further calls wait in arrival order for at most 12 seconds, and that `SERVER_BUSY` means the call was refused before any kDrive request, so it is safe to retry (writes included) once an earlier call has completed. `CHANGELOG.md` `## [Unreleased]` has a `### Fixed` line citing #16.

## Rationale

`SERVER_BUSY` is the documented retryable refusal (F-026 D-3); clients and operators need its meaning and the queue bounds. Source: [F-026 v1](../features/F-026-bounded-invocation-queue.md).
