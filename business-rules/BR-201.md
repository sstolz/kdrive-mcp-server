## Rule

`children` requests `limit=50` with `with=path,parents,capabilities` and the request cursor; it returns the echoed request identity with `status:"complete"`, entries in provider order and `terminal:true` when `has_more` is false, or `next:<cursor>` when `has_more` is true and the cursor is a nonempty string of at most 4,096 bytes; any entry whose `parent_id` differs from the requested directory, more than 50 entries, or any other envelope returns `{status:"incomplete"}`. Verified by: AC-11.

## Rationale

Traversal proofs need complete pages bounded to the navigation limit; truncated or foreign pages must not authorize anything. Source: [F-014 v5](../features/F-014-production-kdrive-adapter.md).

## Examples

- **Given** a folder with 1,001 children
  **When** `children` pages through it
  **Then** twenty-one pages, twenty of 50 entries and one of 1, carry `next` then `terminal`.
- **Given** a page with `has_more` true and no cursor
  **When** `children` runs
  **Then** it returns `{status:"incomplete"}`.
