## Rule

Cancellation means the client's cancellation or session close; after the intent append starts, the navigation 10,000 ms deadline is not a cancellation and does not reach the gate. When the navigation signal is aborted at the moment `dispatch` settles (denied or thrown) and no intent append has started, the run makes zero records (no `denied` record, overriding BR-246) and zero attempts, and the caller gets `REQUEST_TIMEOUT` for the navigation deadline or `CANCELLED` for a cancellation. Cancellation observed after the intent append starts and before the first attempt, or during a 429 wait, ends the run `failed` with code `CANCELLED` and an outcome record (once a pending intent append resolves; if it rejects, BR-244 applies), with no further attempt. Cancellation during an attempt does not abort it, and the attempt is classified and recorded normally.

## Rationale

Cancellation must never turn a dispatched write into a lie, and must stop work not yet started (D-16-9). Source: [F-016 v1](../features/F-016-mutation-audit-gate.md).

## Examples

- **Given** a cancellation after the intent resolved and before the first attempt
  **When** the run continues
  **Then** it ends `failed`/`CANCELLED` with 0 attempts and an outcome record
- **Given** a cancellation during an attempt that answers 200
  **When** the attempt settles
  **Then** the run is `succeeded` and the mutator signal was not aborted
