## Rule

Cancellation, deadline and close terminate owned extraction work before the invocation slot is released and suppress late publication.

v1 (accepted; superseded by v2): the same statement, with the example "a pending read and a concurrent info call → `SERVER_BUSY`".

## Rationale

Cancelling an unresponsive CPU task must not leak a slot or publish late data. Source: [F-009 v1](../features/F-009-bounded-content-reading.md).

## Examples

- **Given** extractTimeoutMs1 and an owned worker.
  **When** its deadline fires.
  **Then** REQUEST_TIMEOUT follows owned worker termination.
- **Given** a pending read and a concurrent info call.
  **When** the second call starts.
  **Then** the info call waits and runs after the read settles (owned worker terminated first), and later slot reuse works (v2; v1: SERVER_BUSY is returned).
- **Given** a pending read and 4 waiting calls (v2).
  **When** a further call starts.
  **Then** SERVER_BUSY is returned and the read's worker lifecycle is unaffected.
