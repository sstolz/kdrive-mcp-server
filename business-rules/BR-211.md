## Rule

`make kdrive-record` runs only inside the dev Compose service, reads the token and the private fixture manifest from the BR-212 directory, issues at most 200 GET requests within 600,000 ms through the production transport, records every children page of each folder in its plan up to the terminal page (21 pages for the 1,001-child folder and at least 6 for the received child folder at 50 entries per page) and fails without writing when a folder is still nonterminal after 32 pages, and writes only sanitized recordings under `artifacts/kdrive-recordings/<run>/`; it writes nothing when the BR-213 leak check fails. On success it prints, per route template (the `shared_with_me` route included), the request count and the maximum request duration in milliseconds measured with a monotonic clock, and nothing that names an ID, name, path, cursor, ETag, URL or token.

## Rationale

Provider semantics must be qualified on real but sanitized responses produced within fixed bounds by the owner. Source: [F-014 v5](../features/F-014-production-kdrive-adapter.md).

## Examples

- **Given** a recording plan
  **When** it runs with a recorder answering one hop per request
  **Then** at most 200 requests are issued.
- **Given** a recorder answering 20 hops per request
  **When** the plan runs
  **Then** it stops with `The recording exceeded 200 requests.` and writes nothing.
- **Given** the 1,001-child folder at 50 entries per page
  **When** the plan runs
  **Then** all 21 pages are recorded; a folder still nonterminal after 32 pages fails the recording and writes nothing.
- **Given** a successful recording whose slowest children page took 1,879 ms
  **When** the recorder prints its summary
  **Then** the children route template shows its request count and a maximum of 1,879 ms, and no ID, name or cursor appears.
