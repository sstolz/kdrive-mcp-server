## Rule

The run's total deadline is 60,000 ms of monotonic time from the start of the first attempt; each upstream attempt is aborted by the gate after the smaller of 30,000 ms and the time left until that deadline, and is then classified `uncertain`.

## Rationale

MCP clients need a bounded call; an aborted attempt has an unknown effect. Source: [F-016 v1](../features/F-016-mutation-audit-gate.md).

## Examples

- **Given** a mutator that never settles
  **When** 30,000 ms pass
  **Then** its signal is aborted and the run is `uncertain`
- **Given** a 429 at 25,000 ms with `Retry-After: 10` and a second attempt that never settles
  **When** the run continues
  **Then** the second attempt is aborted at 60,000 ms and the run is `uncertain` with 2 attempts
