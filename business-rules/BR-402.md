# BR-402 — Consent page layout

## Rule

The consent page title is `Authorize access`. Below the `<h1>` it shows, in this order: (1) the fixed paragraph `An application is asking to connect to this kdrive-mcp-server. The server gives MCP clients, such as AI assistants, access to files in kDrive, limited to the folders and mode its operator configured. Approve only if you started this connection yourself and recognise the client below.`; (2) an `<h2>Request details</h2>` followed by the BR-129 and BR-393 lines, each the text of its own `<p>`, with the same wording, `<code>`/`<strong>` markup, escaping and order as BR-129 v2/BR-393 v1 (CIMD: client ID, client host, client name, metadata notice, loopback line when applicable; registered: client name, self-declared notice, client ID; then redirect URI and protected resource); (3) the unchanged form (method, action, hidden `consent` input, `Approve` then `Deny` buttons with `name="decision"`), the Approve button with `class="button primary"` and Deny with `class="button secondary"`, both sized by the shared `.button` rule; (4) the fixed paragraph `Approve takes you to sign in with your account; the client gets access only after that sign-in. Deny returns to the client without access. Enter your password only on your identity provider's sign-in page, never on this page.`

## Rationale

The user must know where they are, why, and what Approve and Deny do, with the BR-129/BR-393 details unchanged. Source: [F-030 v1](../features/F-030-oauth-proxy-page-design.md).

## Examples

- **Given** a registered client named `Plain`
  **When** authorize shows the consent page
  **Then** intro, `Request details`, the BR-129 lines, the form with `button primary` Approve and `button secondary` Deny, and the after-note appear in that order
- **Given** a CIMD client named `<b>Claude</b>` with `redirect_uri` `http://127.0.0.1:33418/cb`
  **When** authorize shows the consent page
  **Then** the BR-393 lines, the loopback line included, appear escaped and in order under `Request details`
