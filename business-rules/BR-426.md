# BR-426 — Extraction permit lifetime and exact release

## Rule

Every granted extraction permit remains charged until owned source-body
cleanup and confirmed worker exit, then releases exactly once with pipeline
source/intermediate references dropped; failed or overdue termination
transfers the permit and identity charge to a detached exit watcher while
the invocation settles no later than its deadline and releases its session slot.

## Rationale

The permit covers complete-source acquisition and transfer copies, worker
work, result projection and final authorization. Returning it on an abort
signal or worker message alone would admit new work while the old pipeline
still owns resources. Success, source/policy errors, worker construction or
execution failures, invalid output, projection errors, timeout, cancel and
close all share cleanup. Duplicate or late events never release twice or
publish twice. A worker's permit cannot be reused while it still runs,
including after termination rejection; confirmed exit is required. Garbage
collection and RSS reduction are not prerequisites. Source:
[F-040 v1](../features/F-040-process-wide-extraction-admission.md),
ownership details and D-2/D-4. Normal exit-before-slot-release remains;
failed/overdue termination is an explicit proposed exception to BR-118 v2,
whose pending version must be synchronized before freeze. A live termination
rejection settles immediately as `CONTENT_UNAVAILABLE`, matching the existing
extraction failure code. Pending termination at the deadline settles as
`REQUEST_TIMEOUT`, or `ACCESS_DENIED` if cancel/close was already recorded;
a late rejection cannot overwrite that result. No new policy call delays
this fixed lifecycle failure. Close still publishes no wire result.

Install the exit observer before termination and transfer ownership atomically
with an already-exited check. The watcher retains only the worker, minimal
exit/error-drain state and permit/identity token, never invocation/session,
source bytes or output. It releases only on confirmed exit, handles duplicate
or racing events once, and retains its identity charge meanwhile. A worker
that never exits holds its permit indefinitely, but cannot retain the
invocation slot beyond 10 s. At most two detached watchers can exist.

## Examples

Unless explicitly shared, all example controllers have distinct identities.

- **E-01 — Given** two active pipelines and one waiter
  **When** one pipeline successfully returns the four-character text `safe`
  **Then** it closes its body once, terminates its worker and releases once;
  the waiter starts once and returns F-040's exact result `S`. Maps to
  `TestBR426_v1_AC07_every_terminal_path_releases_once`, `success` row.
- **E-02 — Given** the same capacity with one active source read pending
  **When** that read rejects with `ReadFailure(503)`
  **Then** its result is exactly `READ_UNAVAILABLE`, its body closes once
  and its permit is reusable without a worker start for that failed read.
  Maps to `TestBR426_v1_AC07_every_terminal_path_releases_once`,
  `source-error` row.
- **E-03 — Given** an active started worker and one queued extraction
  **When** cancellation requests termination but the exit acknowledgment
  remains held before the invocation deadline
  **Then** the active permit and invocation slot stay held and the waiter
  cannot start. Maps to
  `TestBR426_v1_AC08_worker_exit_precedes_permit_reuse`, `cancel` row.
- **E-04 — Given** E-03's terminated worker
  **When** its owned exit/cleanup acknowledgment completes before the deadline
  **Then** the cancelled call settles once as `ACCESS_DENIED`, the permit
  hands off once and late worker events cannot release it again. Maps to
  `TestBR426_v1_AC08_worker_exit_precedes_permit_reuse`, `cancel` row.
- **E-05 — Given** a permit just granted but content not yet started
  **When** its invocation aborts
  **Then** it starts no content, returns `ACCESS_DENIED` once and hands the
  permit to the next live waiter once. Maps to
  `TestBR425_v1_AC06_dead_waiter_cannot_win_permit_handoff`,
  `abort-after` row, also asserting BR-426 v1.

- **E-06 — Given** slot start t=0, a live worker and two held global permits
  **When** its termination rejects at t=1,000 and it keeps running past t=10,000
  **Then** the call settles at t=1,000 as exactly `CONTENT_UNAVAILABLE`, its
  session slot is released, and the detached watcher keeps its permit and
  identity charged until confirmed exit; no second result occurs. Maps to
  `TestBR426_v1_AC08_worker_exit_precedes_permit_reuse`, `termination-rejection`.
- **E-07 — Given** the same worker with no earlier cancel/close
  **When** termination remains pending at t=10,000
  **Then** exactly `REQUEST_TIMEOUT` settles at t=10,000 and the session slot
  releases while the watcher retains the permit/identity until exit. Maps to
  `TestBR426_v1_AC08_worker_exit_precedes_permit_reuse`,
  `termination-pending-at-deadline` (cancel/close companion rows preserve
  `ACCESS_DENIED`; close suppresses wire publication).
