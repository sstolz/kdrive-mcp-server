# BR-384 — Waiting time is not charged to the invocation

## Rule

An invocation's 10,000 ms deadline, its 512 dependency-call budget, its five-list budget, its output bounds and its pagination state start when it takes the slot; the time spent waiting counts toward none of them.

## Rationale

Every invocation keeps the full bounds it has today; a queued call must not time out because of the call ahead of it. Source: [F-026 v1](../features/F-026-bounded-invocation-queue.md).

## Examples

- **Given** a call that waited 9,000 ms
  **When** its port answers 9,500 ms after it took the slot
  **Then** it succeeds
- **Given** a call that waited 9,000 ms and a port that never answers
  **When** time passes
  **Then** it returns `REQUEST_TIMEOUT` 10,000 ms after it took the slot (19,000 ms after it joined)
- **Given** a first invocation that made 512 dependency calls
  **When** the next invocation runs
  **Then** it can make 512 dependency calls and five list pages
