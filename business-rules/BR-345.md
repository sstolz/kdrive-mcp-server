# BR-345 — Privacy scan of the tracked entries

## Rule

The scanned entries are exactly the output of `git ls-files -z -s` at the checkout. Contents are read only from the object database through one `git cat-file --batch` process by blob id, never from the working tree. A symlink entry (mode `120000`) is scanned as its target text; a gitlink (mode `160000`) is skipped and counted. Decoding is exactly: a UTF-16 LE or BE byte-order mark decodes the rest as UTF-16; otherwise a blob that decodes as strict UTF-8 (a UTF-8 BOM removed) with no NUL byte is text; anything else is binary, skipped and counted. Each text is scanned per line in three views (as is; after `%XX` percent-decoding; after decoding HTML numeric entities `&#NN;` and `&#xHH;`), and once more over the as-is text with line breaks collapsed to single spaces, a hit reported at the line where the match starts (archive `scanText` semantics); one hit per line and pattern.

## Rationale

The tree that becomes public is the committed one; encoded and wrapped forms must not slip past a pattern, and the working tree is not what is published. Source: [F-023 v1](../features/F-023-hosted-ci-privacy-scan.md).

## Examples

- **Given** a file with the token on line 2, `%2D`-encoded on line 4, `&#45;`-encoded on line 5, and `synthetic deny` on line 6 followed by `1234` on line 7
  **When** the scan runs with the two synthetic patterns
  **Then** exactly four hits print (lines 2, 4, 5 pattern 1; line 6 pattern 2) and the exit is 1
- **Given** a file with a NUL byte and one with invalid UTF-8, each holding the token
  **When** the scan runs
  **Then** both are counted in `binary skipped` and no hit prints
- **Given** a committed file deleted from the working tree, a symlink whose target holds the token, and a gitlink
  **When** the scan runs
  **Then** the first two are reported at line 1 and the gitlink is counted in `gitlinks skipped`
