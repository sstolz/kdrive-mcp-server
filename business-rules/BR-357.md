# BR-357 — Release job steps

## Rule

The steps of each `release.yml` job are exactly, in order: `verify`: checkout, `make release-check`, `make release-setup`, `make pack`, `make release-npm` (`id: release-npm`), `make image-binfmt`, `make release-image`, upload of `artifacts/sstolz-kdrive-mcp-server-*.tgz` as artifact `npm-tarball`; `publish-image`: checkout, `make release-check`, `make image-binfmt`, `make release-image`; `publish-npm`: checkout, `make release-check`, download of `npm-tarball` into `artifacts/`, `make release-npm`; `read-back`: checkout, `make release-verify`. Every checkout uses `ref: ${{ github.sha }}`, `fetch-depth: 0` and `persist-credentials: false`; no step checks out or executes `inputs.sha`.

## Rationale

Every publishing job re-checks before it publishes, the npm job publishes the tarball `verify` built, and only the dispatched `main` HEAD code runs. Source: [F-024 v2](../features/F-024-gated-publication.md).
