## Rule

`examples/config.yaml` uses only F-002 YAML keys, sets `transport: http`, `oauthMode: proxy`, `httpHost: 0.0.0.0` and `httpPort: 3000`, carries credentials only through `tokenFile`, `oauthUpstreamClientSecretFile` and `oauthSigningKeyFile` with paths under `/run/secrets/`, and is accepted by F-002 `loadConfiguration` (`--config`) when its credential paths are replaced by temporary synthetic files.

## Rationale

The documented alternative to environment configuration. Source: [F-013 v1](../features/F-013-http-image.md).

## Examples

- **Given** `config.yaml` with credential paths replaced by synthetic files
  **When** `loadConfiguration --config` runs
  **Then** it returns transport `http` and OAuth mode `proxy`
- **Given** `config.yaml`
  **When** the test reads it
  **Then** it has no `token` or `oauthUpstreamClientSecret` key
