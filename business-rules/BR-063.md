## Rule

Metadata output is a fixed scalar projection of the exact normalized live item authorized by the policy invocation.

## Rationale

Arbitrary execute responses or custom metadata fields are not authorization evidence. Source: [F-005 v1](../features/F-005-stdio-navigation.md), AC-05.

## Examples

- **Given** authorized file7:3 at /docs/a.
  **When** info reads file3.
  **Then** only drive_id,id,name,type,path are returned.
- **Given** a metadata object with a getter or unchecked execute canary.
  **When** info runs.
  **Then** the getter/execute payload is never serialized or executed.
