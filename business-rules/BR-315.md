# BR-315 — Pinned dependency overrides

## Rule

v2 (F-027 v2, #12; accepted on main `75bd9bb`): `package.json` has a top-level `overrides` object deep-equal to `{"minimatch": "11.0.8"}`, and `zod` stays a direct `dependencies` entry with an exact version. The two Jimp overrides leave with Jimp (BR-389, ADR-010).

v1:

`package.json` has a top-level `overrides` object deep-equal to `{"@jimp/types": {"zod": "$zod"}, "@jimp/plugin-resize": {"zod": "$zod"}, "minimatch": "11.0.8"}`, and `zod` stays a direct `dependencies` entry with an exact version.

## Rationale

Jimp 1.6.1 nests Zod 3.25.76 (high GHSA-mrhx-c7xc-m3w5, no fixed 3.x) and the tooling resolves `minimatch` 10.2.6 (moderate); the overrides remove both at the source without forking Jimp (D-1, D-2, ADR-009). Source: [F-020 v1](../features/F-020-dependency-audit.md).
