## Rule

An invalid, expired or unavailable continuation produces one fixed sanitized error and starts no dependency call.

## Rationale

Invalid continuations must not disclose state or start protected work. Source: [F-006 v1](../features/F-006-filtered-pagination.md).

## Examples

- **Given** a handle age299999ms.
  **When** it resumes with its bound arguments.
  **Then** the handle remains usable.
- **Given** a handle age300000ms.
  **When** it resumes.
  **Then** INVALID_CURSOR occurs without protected dependency work.
