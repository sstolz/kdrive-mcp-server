## Rule

A gate result contains only `result`, `op` (absent for an admission refusal), `code` (absent on `succeeded`), `attempts`, `status` (last HTTP status, when one was received), `audit`, `message` (fixed strings only) and, on `succeeded`, the mutator's validated value.

## Rationale

A fixed result shape keeps gate output free of provider or exception text. Source: [F-016 v1](../features/F-016-mutation-audit-gate.md).

## Examples

- **Given** every result type
  **When** results are returned
  **Then** their members are a subset of the listed ones
- **Given** an `uncertain` result
  **When** it is returned
  **Then** its `message` is the BR-251 fixed string
