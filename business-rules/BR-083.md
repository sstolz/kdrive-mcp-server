## Rule

Raw continuation offsets count bytes, and every successful chunk is bound to one qualified strong file version.

## Rationale

Byte-based chunks must never combine versions or confuse encoded length with raw position. Source: [F-007 v1](../features/F-007-bounded-downloads.md).

## Examples

- **Given** the known binary is seven bytes and length_bytes is2.
  **When** successive chunks are decoded and their byte offsets resumed with the strong ETag.
  **Then** the exact seven original bytes are reconstructed.
- **Given** the supplied expected ETag differs from the qualified descriptor.
  **When** continuation is requested.
  **Then** VERSION_CHANGED occurs before range opening.
