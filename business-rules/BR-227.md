## Rule

v3 (F-023 v1, #1, pending acceptance; v2 below stays the accepted rule until then): v2 holds with these changes: every job except `changes`, `privacy-scan`, `fork-refusal` and `ci-result` depends on `changes` directly or through `needs`; `ci-result` needs every other job except `fork-refusal` (adding `privacy-scan`), and its step `if` is exactly `needs.changes.result != 'success' || needs.privacy-scan.result != 'success' || contains(needs.*.result, 'failure') || contains(needs.*.result, 'cancelled')`.

v2 (F-020 v1, #104; accepted on main `b6acf86`, 2026-11-11): v1 holds; `audit` has `needs: [changes]` and the heavy `if` of the gate jobs, and `ci-result` needs every job except `fork-refusal`, `audit` included.

The `changes` job has no `needs`, the job `if` exactly `github.event_name != 'pull_request' || github.event.pull_request.head.repo.full_name == github.repository`, outputs `heavy` and `image` from its step `classify`, and steps: the pinned checkout with `fetch-depth: 2` and `persist-credentials: false`, then `make ci-changes` (id `classify`). `lint`, `format`, `typecheck`, `test` and `build` have `needs: [changes]` and the job `if` exactly `needs.changes.outputs.heavy != 'false'`. Every self-hosted job except `changes` and `ci-result` depends on `changes` directly or through `needs`, and only `ci-result` uses `always()` at job level. `ci-result` needs every other job except `fork-refusal`, has the job `if` exactly `always() && (github.event_name != 'pull_request' || github.event.pull_request.head.repo.full_name == github.repository)`, no checkout, and one step with the `if` exactly `needs.changes.result != 'success' || contains(needs.*.result, 'failure') || contains(needs.*.result, 'cancelled')` whose `run` prints a fixed message to stderr and exits 1.

## Rationale

Fork PRs never reach the self-hosted runner, and one aggregate job carries the merge criterion (T3, D-08). Source: [F-015 v1](../features/F-015-self-hosted-runner.md).

## Examples

- **Given** the real `ci.yml`
  **When** the validator runs
  **Then** `changes`, the gate jobs and `ci-result` have exactly their BR-227 `needs`, `if` and steps
- **Given** a gate job with `needs.changes.outputs.heavy == 'true'`
  **When** the validator runs
  **Then** it is rejected
- **Given** `ci-result` without `privacy-scan` in `needs`

  **Then** it is rejected (v3)
