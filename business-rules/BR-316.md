# BR-316 — Lockfile resolves one Zod 4 and a fixed minimatch

## Rule

In `package-lock.json`, the only `packages` key whose last `node_modules/` segment is `zod` is `node_modules/zod`, and its version is `>=4.6.6`; every `packages` entry whose last `node_modules/` segment is `minimatch` has a version `>=11.0.1`.

## Rationale

The overrides of BR-315 only matter if the committed lockfile, which `npm ci` and the container image install, actually holds the fixed versions. Source: [F-020 v1](../features/F-020-dependency-audit.md).

- **Given** the regenerated lockfile with `node_modules/zod` 4.6.7 and `minimatch` 11.0.8

- **Given** a lockfile with a `minimatch` at 10.2.6
