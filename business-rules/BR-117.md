## Rule

All input, decoded/decompressed, structural, processing-time and serialized-output ceilings are cumulative within one invocation and equality is permitted.

## Rationale

Independent logical, structural, time and output limits prevent multiplied resource work. Source: [F-009 v1](../features/F-009-bounded-content-reading.md).

## Examples

- **Given** Euro decoded from one Windows1252 byte with budget3.
  **When** decoding runs.
  **Then** the exact UTF8 ceiling is accepted.
- **Given** the same source with budget2.
  **When** decoding runs.
  **Then** LIMIT_EXCEEDED is returned.
