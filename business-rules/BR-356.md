# BR-356 — Release job guards, permissions and needs

## Rule

Every `release.yml` job has `runs-on: ubuntu-24.04` and `timeout-minutes: 60`. `verify` has the `if` exactly `github.repository == 'sstolz/kdrive-mcp-server' && github.ref == 'refs/heads/main'`, `permissions` exactly `{contents: read, actions: read, packages: read}`, no `needs` or `environment`, and the output `tarball-sha256` from step `release-npm`. The other jobs have that condition followed by ` && inputs.dry_run == false`; `publish-image` has `needs: [verify]`, `environment: release` and `{contents: read, actions: read, packages: write}`; `publish-npm` has `needs: [verify, publish-image]`, `environment: release` and `{contents: read, actions: read, packages: read, id-token: write}`; `read-back` has `needs: [verify, publish-npm]`, no `environment` and `{contents: read, packages: read}`. Only `publish-image` has `packages: write` and only `publish-npm` has `id-token: write`.

## Rationale

Each publishing credential is held by exactly one job behind the owner's `release` environment approval, and only on `main` of this repository. Source: [F-024 v2](../features/F-024-gated-publication.md).
