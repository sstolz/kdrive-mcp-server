# BR-301 — Identity re-check at refresh

## Rule

After BR-140 v2 step (3) and before its step (4), a refresh request that passed steps (1)–(3) is refused with 400 `invalid_grant`, and its family revoked (one `refresh_family_revoked` event), when the family's identity no longer satisfies the current subject and email allowlists under the F-010 rule used at the callback (`identity_refused`); no token is issued.

## Rationale

Removing a subject or an email from the allowlist must end its refresh families at the next refresh. Source: [F-019 v1](../features/F-019-persistent-oauth-state.md).

## Examples

- **Given** a family whose subject was removed from the allowlist
  **When** it is refreshed
  **Then** 400 `invalid_grant`, the family revoked and no token issued
- **Given** an email family reopened from the file and then removed from `oauthAllowedEmails`
  **When** it is refreshed
  **Then** 400 `invalid_grant` and one `refresh_family_revoked` event
