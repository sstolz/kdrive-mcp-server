## Rule

For `refresh_token`, after BR-136, checks run in this order: (1) an absent or unknown token, or a token of a revoked family, yields 400 `invalid_grant`; (2) a rotated token, or a token presented by another client than its family's, revokes every token of its family and yields 400 `invalid_grant`, whether or not it is also expired; (3) an expired token (BR-139) yields 400 `invalid_grant` and marks its family revoked, so later tokens of it hit step (1) and at most one `refresh_family_revoked` event is logged per family; (4) a `resource` that is present and differs from the family's yields 400 `invalid_target` without rotation. The rotation is committed before the first `await` of the request, so two concurrent presentations of one token produce one success and one family revocation; the winner's new refresh token then belongs to a revoked family and is refused (RFC 9700 §4.14.2). Success returns 200 with the same members as BR-137 success: a new access token (BR-138) for the family's identity and client, `token_type` `Bearer`, `expires_in` `3600` and the new `refresh_token`. Already issued access tokens stay valid until their `exp`.

Version 2 (F-019 v1, #103; accepted on main `68209fe`, 2026-11-11): step (1): a malformed token, an unknown family ID or a revoked family yields 400 `invalid_grant`; step (2): a known family whose current secret hash differs (constant-time comparison) or that belongs to another client is revoked and yields 400 `invalid_grant`; steps (3)–(4), concurrency and success are unchanged; BR-301 runs after step (3) and before step (4).

## Rationale

Refresh-token reuse detection (RFC 9700 §4.14.2) limits a stolen token to one use before the whole family dies. Source: [F-010 v1](../features/F-010-oauth-proxy.md), AC-21, AC-22.

## Examples

- **Given** R1 rotated into R2
  **When** R1 is presented again
  **Then** 400 `invalid_grant` and R2 is refused too.
- **Given** two concurrent presentations of R1
  **When** both complete
  **Then** one success, one revocation, and the winner's token is refused.
