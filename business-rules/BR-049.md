# BR-049 — Shared-root membership

## Rule

An external target is authorized only when its exact same-drive identity is a discovered received-share root or a complete validated fresh chain connects it to such a root.

The detailed frozen evidence, capability, cache and effect contracts in
[F-004 v1](../features/F-004-received-share-policy.md) are normative.

## Rationale

A known external ID or capability alone cannot establish membership.
