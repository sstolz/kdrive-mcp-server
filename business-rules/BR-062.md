## Rule

Drive enumeration exposes only configured drives and their currently authorized root descriptors.

## Rationale

Configured-drive discovery cannot expand into external shares or denied roots. Source: [F-005 v1](../features/F-005-stdio-navigation.md), AC-04.

## Examples

- **Given** configured drives7 and9 with permitted roots.
  **When** list_drives omits drive_id.
  **Then** both permitted descriptors appear in configuration order.
- **Given** drive7 root /docs denied by policy.
  **When** list_drives runs.
  **Then** the denied root and empty drive descriptor are omitted.
