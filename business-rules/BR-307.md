# BR-307 — CIMD address policy

## Rule

An address is allowed only when it is (a) an IPv4 address outside every range of Contract details "Blocked IPv4 ranges", or (b) an IPv6 address inside `2000::/3` and outside every range of Contract details "Blocked IPv6 ranges". Every other IPv6 address is blocked, which includes the unspecified and loopback addresses, IPv4-mapped (`::ffff:0:0/96`), IPv4-translated (`::ffff:0:0:0/96`), IPv4-compatible (`::/96`), the NAT64 prefixes `64:ff9b::/96` and `64:ff9b:1::/48`, unique-local, link-local, site-local and multicast addresses, whatever address they embed.

## Rationale

Pinning to checked public addresses stops DNS rebinding to private, loopback and special-use ranges; allowing only `2000::/3` for IPv6 removes every embedded-IPv4 form (D-9). Source: [F-028 v1](../features/F-028-client-id-metadata-documents.md).

## Examples

- **Given** the resolved address `10.1.2.3`, `::ffff:8.8.8.8` or `2002:808:808::1`
  **When** the address policy classifies it
  **Then** it is blocked
- **Given** the resolved address `8.8.8.8`, `2606:4700::1` or `2001:200::1`
  **When** the address policy classifies it
  **Then** it is allowed
- **Given** a resolver answering `[8.8.8.8, 127.0.0.1]`
  **When** the fetch runs
  **Then** it fails as `blocked_address` and no socket is opened
