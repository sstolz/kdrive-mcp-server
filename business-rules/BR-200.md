## Rule

`children` with location `original-trash` and the adapter's own `execute` return `{status:"unavailable"}` without any upstream request; production binds the policy `execute` to the gate (BR-256 unchanged). Verified by: AC-09.

## Rationale

Trash evidence and execution are out of scope; returning unavailable without requests keeps writes impossible. Source: [F-014 v1](../features/F-014-production-kdrive-adapter.md).

  **Then** it returns `{status:"unavailable"}` without any request.
- **Given** the adapter's `execute({kind:"rename"})`

  **Then** it returns `{status:"unavailable"}` without any request.
