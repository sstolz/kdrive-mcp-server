## Rule

Image decoding starts only after bounded format/dimension/ decompression admission and publishes exactly one complete valid resized PNG with metadata, or an explicit refusal.

## Rationale

MCP image blocks must contain complete independent images instead of raw byte slices. Source: [F-009 v1](../features/F-009-bounded-content-reading.md).

## Examples

- **Given** a20x10 baseline JPEG.
  **When** image extraction runs.
  **Then** one complete20x10 PNG is emitted without upscaling.
- **Given** a JPEG declaring1049600 pixels.
  **When** admission runs.
  **Then** LIMIT_EXCEEDED occurs before decode.
