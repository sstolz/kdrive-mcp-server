# BR-302 — Purge of expired state and disallowed clients

## Rule

Expired state is purged at startup and before each write, in memory and in the file: a family whose absolute or inactivity expiry (BR-139 v2) has passed, and a dynamically registered client whose last use is 30 days or more in the past and that has no live family. A revoked family is deleted in the transaction that revokes it. The startup purge also deletes, with its families, every registered client none of whose stored redirect URIs matches an entry of the current `oauthAllowedRedirectUris` under BR-126.

## Rationale

Persisted state must not outlive its expiry or the redirect URIs the operator allows. Source: [F-019 v1](../features/F-019-persistent-oauth-state.md).

## Examples

- **Given** a family past its absolute expiry
  **When** the store opens
  **Then** the family is deleted from memory and file
- **Given** a client last used 30 days ago with no live family
  **When** a write happens
  **Then** the client is deleted first
- **Given** a client none of whose redirect URIs is in the current allowlist
  **When** the store opens
  **Then** the client and its families are deleted
