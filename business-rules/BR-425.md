# BR-425 — Extraction waiting obeys invocation liveness

## Rule

Extraction waiting uses the running invocation's existing 10,000 ms
deadline and cancellation state, removing expired waiters with
`REQUEST_TIMEOUT` and cancelled/closed waiters with `ACCESS_DENIED` before
they can acquire a permit or start content work, without resetting the
deadline at grant.

## Rationale

Unlike the F-026 per-session queue, this queue is entered after the
invocation owns its session slot. Queue time must not extend its deadline.
The worker timeout remains the smaller of `extractTimeoutMs` and invocation
time remaining at worker creation. Removal frees queue capacity and removes
its timer/listener state; a closed session publishes no wire response.
Enqueue and handoff recheck liveness, including monotonic expiry even when
its timer has not fired. A recorded invocation abort cause keeps the
existing controller's precedence. If abort wins just after grant, BR-426
releases the permit without content work. Source:
[F-040 v1](../features/F-040-process-wide-extraction-admission.md),
time/observable outcomes and D-3.

## Examples

- **E-01 — Given** slot start at t=0 and extraction queue entry at t=2,000
  **When** time is t=9,999 ms with no permit available
  **Then** the call still waits and starts no content. Maps to
  `TestBR425_v1_AC04_queue_and_work_share_invocation_deadline`, `queued` row.
- **E-02 — Given** the same waiting invocation
  **When** time reaches t=10,000 ms
  **Then** it returns exactly the `REQUEST_TIMEOUT` tool error, is removed
  and never starts after a later release. Maps to
  `TestBR425_v1_AC04_queue_and_work_share_invocation_deadline`, `queued` row.
- **E-03 — Given** a permit granted at t=9,000 ms and a pending body read
  **When** time reaches t=10,000 ms from session-slot acquisition
  **Then** it returns `REQUEST_TIMEOUT`, aborts the read signal and closes
  the body once. Maps to
  `TestBR425_v1_AC04_queue_and_work_share_invocation_deadline`, `body` row.
- **E-04 — Given** four queued callers and two active extractions
  **When** one queued caller aborts
  **Then** it settles exactly once as `ACCESS_DENIED`, starts no content,
  leaves the others intact and frees the fourth queue place. Maps to
  `TestBR425_v1_AC05_queued_cancel_and_close_remove_only_the_owner`,
  `cancel` row.
- **E-05 — Given** a waiter at its deadline and its timer not yet fired
  **When** a permit becomes available
  **Then** it gets exactly `REQUEST_TIMEOUT`, starts no content and cannot
  take capacity from the next live waiter. Maps to
  `TestBR425_v1_AC06_dead_waiter_cannot_win_permit_handoff`,
  `deadline-before` row.
