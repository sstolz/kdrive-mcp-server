## Rule

Cancellation, timeout, close or a failed page invalidates the affected traversal and publishes no partial batch or reusable continuation.

## Rationale

Failed or abandoned operations must not leave partial success or reusable state. Source: [F-006 v1](../features/F-006-filtered-pagination.md).

## Examples

- **Given** an admitted continuation and never-settling provider.
  **When** the invocation is cancelled.
  **Then** no batch/new cursor publishes and the consumed handle is invalid.
- **Given** a response would exceed the configured output limit.
  **When** the continuation runs.
  **Then** LIMIT_EXCEEDED returns and that traversal is invalidated.
