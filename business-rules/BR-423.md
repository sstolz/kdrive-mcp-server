# BR-423 — Process-wide extraction permits before buffering

## Rule

Every `kdrive_read` extraction pipeline holds one of exactly two process-wide
permits before entering complete-source acquisition, shared across all
controllers, sessions and formats in the parent process, including empty
sources and continuation calls; each trusted identity holds at most one
permit, including permits transferred to detached exit watchers.

## Rationale

The 64-session ceiling multiplies per-call source buffers and workers; worker
heap limits do not bound external memory. Admission before `content.complete`
covers `describe`, `open`, reads, source allocation and transfer copies as
well as the worker. Raw downloads, resource reads and metadata do not take
these permits. A grant conveys no authority: fresh policy checks still run.
Source: [F-040 v1](../features/F-040-process-wide-extraction-admission.md),
contract details and D-1/D-2. This is a new aggregate constraint; existing
per-invocation byte/time bounds remain unchanged. Identity comes from trusted
composition: equal OAuth issuer/subject pairs share one allowance across
sessions; stdio and missing-identity embedded controllers share one stdio
identity, and insecure HTTP sessions share one distinct insecure identity.

## Examples

Unless explicitly shared, all example controllers have distinct identities.

- **E-01 — Given** one controller holding a permit during a source read
  **When** a second controller with a different identity starts an extraction
  **Then** it opens its source and the two pipelines can proceed concurrently.
  Maps to `TestBR423_v1_AC01_third_extraction_waits_before_source_buffering`,
  first two calls in the `reads held` case.
- **E-02 — Given** two controllers holding permits during source reads
  **When** a third controller starts an extraction
  **Then** it waits with zero describe/open/read calls, source allocations,
  copies or worker starts until one complete pipeline releases its permit.
  Maps to `TestBR423_v1_AC01_third_extraction_waits_before_source_buffering`,
  third call in the `reads held` case.
- **E-03 — Given** two active text workers
  **When** another controller requests PDF extraction
  **Then** its content/worker starts stay at zero until grant; after grant
  its result equals the characterized unsaturated PDF result byte for byte.
  Maps to `TestBR423_v1_AC10_formats_share_capacity_and_other_reads_do_not`,
  `pdf` row.
- **E-04 — Given** two active extractions
  **When** a different controller downloads raw bytes
  **Then** it returns its characterized raw payload without waiting for an
  extraction permit. Maps to
  `TestBR423_v1_AC10_formats_share_capacity_and_other_reads_do_not`,
  `kdrive_download` row.
- **E-05 — Given** a queued extraction whose read authority is revoked
  **When** it receives a permit
  **Then** fresh policy refuses with `ACCESS_DENIED`, no describe/open/worker
  starts, and the permit is released. Maps to
  `TestBR423_v1_AC09_granted_waiter_rechecks_authority`.

- **E-06 — Given** identity A holds a permit in one HTTP session
  **When** another session of A requests extraction while a global permit is free
  **Then** the second call waits without source work until A releases. A
  different eligible identity can use the spare permit. Maps to
  `TestBR423_v1_AC11_identity_cap_and_oldest_eligible_waiter`,
  `same-identity`, `eight-http-sessions` and `identity-key` rows; the same
  test covers stdio/fallback and insecure HTTP composition.
