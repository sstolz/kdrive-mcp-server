# BR-275 — Single-drive move

## Rule

`kdrive_move` has a single `drive_id` for its source and destination, and a move operation whose source and destination evidence carry different drive IDs is denied with zero intent records and zero mutation requests.

## Rationale

Cross-drive moves are out of scope and unmeasured. Source: [F-017 v1](../features/F-017-controlled-tree-mutations.md).

## Examples

- **Given** `kdrive_move` with `destination_drive_id`
  **When** the tool is called
  **Then** it returns `INVALID_ARGUMENTS`
- **Given** a destination whose evidence carries drive 202 for a move on drive 101
  **When** the tool is called
  **Then** the result is `denied` with no intent record and no write
