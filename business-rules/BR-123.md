## Rule

**v2 (F-014 v1, pending acceptance).** Missing qualified content dependencies refuse authorized reads explicitly while raw reads, resources and navigation keep their accepted contracts; the production composition always supplies the content port.

v1 below stays the accepted rule until v2 is accepted on actual main.

### v1 (accepted)

Missing qualified content dependencies refuse authorized reads explicitly while existing raw/resources/navigation and the production diagnostic retain their accepted contracts.

## Rationale

Injected codec functionality cannot be mistaken for a qualified production kDrive runtime. Source: [F-009 v1](../features/F-009-bounded-content-reading.md).

## Examples

- **Given** an authorized file with no content port.
  **When** kdrive_read runs.
  **Then** CONTENT_UNAVAILABLE is explicit.
- **Given** an unauthorized file with no content port.
  **When** the same read runs.
  **Then** ACCESS_DENIED precedes unavailable diagnostics.
