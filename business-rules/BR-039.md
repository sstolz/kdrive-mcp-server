# BR-039 — Original-trash restore proof

## Rule

A restore operation carries only `target`; the policy core takes its destination as the immediate parent of the original-trash evidence (root `1` when the original chain is the root sentinel only), inspects it as `live`, and requires the original-trash membership/path proof, an authorized live directory destination whose ID and path equal that immediate parent's, and an authorized result path; any difference between the two reads is `denied`; a restore operation with `destination` or `name` is refused `INVALID_OPERATION`; no server-side substitute for missing trash evidence is stored.

This amends F-003's evidence contract: the original parent chain is the former parent's chain as read live at restore time (F-017 BR-264), not a deletion-time record, and F-003's sentences "Original parent fields describe their identities and paths at deletion" and "Future restore evidence must include the original full chain" no longer apply to it. The other normalized evidence, matching, effect and paging details in [F-003 v1](../features/F-003-tree-access-policy.md) remain normative.

## Rationale

Avoid granting restore authority from a current trash location or an allowed destination alone.

## Examples

These are synthetic injected cases, not provider observations.

- **Given** original-trash evidence for `/r/a/report` whose immediate parent is live folder 11 at `/r/a`
  **When** the policy decides `{kind:"restore", target}`
  **Then** it is authorized with folder 11 as destination.
- **Given** the same evidence but a live read of folder 11 at `/r/a2`
  **When** the policy decides the restore
  **Then** ACCESS_DENIED is returned.
- **Given** a restore operation carrying `destination`
  **When** the policy checks it
  **Then** it is refused `INVALID_OPERATION`.
