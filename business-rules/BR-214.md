## Rule

CI runs the adapter contract suite against the committed recordings through an injected transport with no network access, and a test refuses committed recordings that contain a member outside the Recorded-members list or a value outside the BR-213 v3 synthetic forms (including a cursor other than `cursor<k>` or an ETag other than `"etag<k>"`).

## Rationale

CI must exercise the adapter on recorded provider shapes without network access or credentials, and refuse unsanitized recordings. Source: [F-014 v3](../features/F-014-production-kdrive-adapter.md).

## Examples

- **Given** the committed recordings
  **When** `make check` runs
  **Then** the contract suite replays them through the injected transport with complete results.
- **Given** a recording with a planted `created_by` member
  **When** the recordings test runs
  **Then** it is refused.
- **Given** a recording with a cursor `cursor-1` or an ETag `"etag-1"`
  **When** the recordings test runs
  **Then** it is refused.
