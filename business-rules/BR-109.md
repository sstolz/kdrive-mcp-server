## Rule

Each read download attempt and each final publication passes the existing fresh owner-drive/file read policy, with no protected content call or content disclosure after denial.

## Rationale

Revocation and owner-drive isolation apply to extracted content as well as raw bytes. Source: [F-009 v1](../features/F-009-bounded-content-reading.md).

## Examples

- **Given** a readable received file8:24.
  **When** kdrive_read extracts text.
  **Then** fresh policy permits publication.
- **Given** the same share revoked during worker execution.
  **When** the result is ready.
  **Then** ACCESS_DENIED discards the result.
