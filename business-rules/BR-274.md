# BR-274 — Production mutator registry

## Rule

The production composition registers exactly the mutators for `create`, `rename`, `move`, `trash`, `restore`, `upload-new` and `replace`; a run of any other kind is refused at admission (BR-245).

## Rationale

Upload and sharing arrive with #19–#20; their kinds stay unavailable. Source: [F-017 v1](../features/F-017-controlled-tree-mutations.md).

## Examples

- **Given** the production composition in mode `write`
  **When** the gate is built
  **Then** its mutators are exactly the five kinds
- **Given** a run of kind `upload-new`
  **When** it is admitted
  **Then** it is refused `UNAVAILABLE` with zero records
