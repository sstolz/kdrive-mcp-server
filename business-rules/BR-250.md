## Rule

**v1 (F-016 v1; active until v2 acceptance)**

Cancellation means the client's cancellation or session close; after the intent append starts, the navigation 10,000 ms deadline is not a cancellation and does not reach the gate. When the navigation signal is aborted at the moment `dispatch` settles (denied or thrown) and no intent append has started, the run makes zero records (no `denied` record, overriding BR-246) and zero attempts, and the caller gets `REQUEST_TIMEOUT` for the navigation deadline or `CANCELLED` for a cancellation. Cancellation observed after the intent append starts and before the first attempt, or during a 429 wait, ends the run `failed` with code `CANCELLED` and an outcome record (once a pending intent append resolves; if it rejects, BR-244 applies), with no further attempt. Cancellation during an attempt does not abort it, and the attempt is classified and recorded normally.

**v2 (F-037 v1, #47 lot A, pending acceptance; v1 stays the active rule until then)** — v1 holds except that there is no 429 wait or cancellation branch after a 429. Cancellation during the sole upstream attempt does not abort it or replace its classification, including `failed` / `RATE_LIMITED`, status 429, attempts 1 on a valid 429 under BR-248 v2; cancellation after that classification likewise cannot replace it. Pre-intent and post-intent/pre-attempt rules, the navigation deadline boundary and BR-244 intent-failure handling stay unchanged.

## Rationale

Cancellation must never turn a dispatched write into a lie, and must stop work not yet started (D-16-9). Source: [F-016 v1](../features/F-016-mutation-audit-gate.md).

v2 follows BR-248 v2's removal of automatic retries: there is no pending retry to cancel, and the sole attempt keeps its classified outcome. Source: [F-037 v1](../features/F-037-mutation-rate-limit-stop.md), D-2–D-3.

## Examples

- **Given** a cancellation after the intent resolved and before the first attempt
  **When** the run continues
  **Then** it ends `failed`/`CANCELLED` with 0 attempts and an outcome record
- **Given** a cancellation during an attempt that answers 200
  **When** the attempt settles
  **Then** the run is `succeeded` and the mutator signal was not aborted

Pending v2 examples (the preceding examples remain valid):

- **Given** a started attempt and a client cancellation before it returns a valid 429
  **When** the reply and outcome append settle
  **Then** the attempt was not aborted; the result is `failed` / `RATE_LIMITED`, attempts 1, status 429, with exactly an intent and matching failed outcome. Maps to `TestBR250_v2_AC05_cancellation_%s_does_not_replace_a_429`, row `during_attempt`.
- **Given** the same 429 classified while its outcome append is held
  **When** cancellation arrives and the append is released
  **Then** the same result and records are returned, with no second attempt. Maps to that test's `during_outcome` row.
- **Given** cancellation after intent resolution but before an attempt
  **When** the run continues
  **Then** it remains `failed` / `CANCELLED`, attempts 0, with an intent and outcome and no status. Maps to `TestBR250_v2_AC05_pre_attempt_cancellation_keeps_its_boundary`.
