# BR-037 — Traversal ceilings and termination

## Rule

A descendant proof that reaches `maxTreeItems` examined descendant entries, exceeds its page-request budget, or cannot prove paging termination produces a denial.

The normalized evidence, matching, effect and paging details in
[F-003 v1](../features/F-003-tree-access-policy.md) remain normative.

## Rationale

Refuse partial proofs and preserve a finite request/entry budget.

## Examples

These are synthetic injected cases, not provider observations.

- **Given** N=3 and a complete two-descendant subtree, **When** trashing its permitted directory, **Then** the effect is authorized.
- **Given** N=3 and a three-descendant subtree, **When** trashing its permitted directory, **Then** ACCESS_DENIED is returned on the third entry.

Both examples map to the test or parameter rows named below.
