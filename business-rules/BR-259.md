## Rule

A file-sink append that has not resolved 5,000 ms of monotonic time after its write started rejects and latches the sink (BR-241); an intent append timeout then gives BR-244 and an outcome append timeout gives BR-252. The stderr sink has no append time limit (D-16-7), but on either sink an intent append that resolves more than 5,000 ms after it started gives zero attempts and an outcome record `failed` with code `AUDIT_UNAVAILABLE`, so a write is never dispatched long after its policy decision.

## Rationale

A stalled disk must not hold a write or dispatch it long after its decision (D-16-7). Source: [F-016 v1](../features/F-016-mutation-audit-gate.md).

## Examples

- **Given** a file sink whose `fdatasync` never settles
  **When** an intent append is 5,000 ms old
  **Then** it rejects, the sink latches and the run returns `AUDIT_UNAVAILABLE`
- **Given** a stderr intent callback firing at 6,000 ms
  **When** the run continues
  **Then** it makes zero attempts and records outcome `failed`/`AUDIT_UNAVAILABLE`
