# BR-047 — Explicit opt-in

## Rule

With `sharedWithMe=false`, received-share discovery and every received-share operation deny without received-share evidence or protected dispatch.

The detailed frozen evidence, capability, cache and effect contracts in
[F-004 v1](../features/F-004-received-share-policy.md) are normative.

## Rationale

Avoid accidental owner-drive exposure from a broad token.

## Examples

These are synthetic injected examples, not provider observations.

- **Given** sharedWithMe=false and known shared child8:21, **When** reading the child, **Then** ACCESS_DENIED is returned with zero received evidence calls.
- **Given** sharedWithMe=true and complete permitted root8:20/child8:21 proofs, **When** reading the child, **Then** the protected synthetic read runs once.

Each example maps to the named parameter row/group below, in the same order.
