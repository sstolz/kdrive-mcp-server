## Rule

The central retry wrapper applies to injected read ports only and never changes abstract policy dispatch or retries `execute`.

## Rationale

Runtime read retry composition cannot silently replay abstract effects. Source: [F-007 v1](../features/F-007-bounded-downloads.md).

## Examples

- **Given** abstract policy dispatch calls execute and execute rejects with branded503.
  **When** the dispatch is awaited.
  **Then** execute is called exactly once and its failure propagates.
- **Given** an abstract inspect fails with branded503.
  **When** policy.check runs outside runtime composition.
  **Then** the failure denies after one inspect attempt, without retry.
