# BR-416 — Content projection

## Rule

A selected page's content is its `Contents` stream, or the streams of a `Contents` array of at most 1,024 references joined with one line feed, tokenized once in order. Kept, with the F-009 operand checks: `q`, `Q`, `cm`, `BT`, `ET`, `Tf`, `Tc`, `Tw`, `Tz`, `TL`, `Ts`, `Tr` (integer 0–7), `Td`, `TD`, `Tm`, `T*`, `Tj`, `TJ`, `'` and `"`, with F-009's state rules except that string bytes are not restricted to 0x20–0xFF and `Tf` takes any font name and a finite size. Dropped, operands included: path construction and painting, clipping, colour and colour space, line and rendering state, `sh`, marked content (`BMC`, `BDC`, `EMC`, `MP`, `DP`), `BX`/`EX`, `gs` (refused when the named ExtGState holds `/Font`), and `Do` of an Image XObject or of a Form XObject whose content, checked with these same rules through nested Forms (at most 8 deep, at most 256 distinct Forms, each decoded once, a Form reached again on its own path is a cycle), shows no text. Refused as `UNSUPPORTED_FORMAT`: `Do` of a Form that shows text, inline images (`BI`), `d0`, `d1` and any other operator. A `Q` with no open `q` is dropped, and `Q`s are appended for the `q`s left open at the end. The kept tokens are re-serialized; that stream counts against the canonical bound.

## Rationale

Common content carries graphics, colour and marked content around text (F-034 S12, S13, S18, S19). Keeping only text operators admits it while PDF.js executes each kept operator once; Forms that show text refuse instead of being dropped silently (F-034 D-4). Source: [F-034 v1](../features/F-034-pdf-extraction-profile.md).

## Examples

- **Given** text between `rg`, `re f` and `BDC`/`EMC` operators
  **When** page 1 is read
  **Then** its text, and the canonical content holds only kept operators
- **Given** a page that shows text through a Form XObject
  **When** page 1 is read
  **Then** `UNSUPPORTED_FORMAT` before PDF.js
