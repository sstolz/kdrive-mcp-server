## Rule

The existing `.dockerignore` (which also feeds the `Dockerfile.dev` build) is kept and contains at least `.git`, `node_modules`, `secrets/`, `.env`, `.env.*`, `*.pem`, `*.key`, `coverage/` and `runs/`. The `Dockerfile` declares no `ARG` or `ENV` whose name contains `TOKEN`, `SECRET`, `PASSWORD` or `KEY`.

## Rationale

Secrets never enter a build context or an image layer. Source: [F-013 v1](../features/F-013-http-image.md).

- **Given** a `.dockerignore` without `runs/`

  **Then** it fails
- **Given** an `ARG KDRIVE_TOKEN` in `runtime`
  **When** the static policy runs
  **Then** it fails
