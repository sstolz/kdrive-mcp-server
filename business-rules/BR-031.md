# BR-031 — Identity-bound roots

## Rule

Resolved path roots remain bound to their preflighted `(driveId, folderId)` until a new startup; a rename, move or path replacement does not rebind them.

The normalized evidence, matching, effect and paging details in
[F-003 v1](../features/F-003-tree-access-policy.md) remain normative.

## Rationale

Keep resolved path roots bound across external moves and replacements.

## Examples

These are synthetic injected cases, not provider observations.

- **Given** bound root 10 externally moved to /else/new with child 13, **When** reading child 13, **Then** the read remains authorized.
- **Given** new folder 99 occupying the old /r path, **When** reading folder 99, **Then** ACCESS_DENIED is returned.

Both examples map to the test or parameter rows named below.
