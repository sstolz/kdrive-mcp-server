# BR-347 — Privacy scan of pull-request text

## Rule

Only when `PRIVACY_EVENT` is `pull_request`, `PRIVACY_PR_TITLE` and `PRIVACY_PR_BODY` are scanned by the BR-345 views as the sources `pr-title` and `pr-body`; on other events they are ignored, whatever their value.

## Rationale

The squash message is built from the PR title and description, so scanning them makes the PR check preventive (D-6). Source: [F-023 v1](../features/F-023-hosted-ci-privacy-scan.md).

## Examples

- **Given** the token in the title and on body line 3
  **When** the scan runs on `pull_request`
  **Then** `pr-title:1` and `pr-body:3` are reported
- **Given** the same values
  **When** the scan runs on `push`
  **Then** no `pr-` hit is reported and the exit is 0

## Verification

AC-10: tests/privacy-scan.test.ts — `BR-347 scans the title and body on pull_request`, `BR-347 ignores the title and body on push`. Run with Docker/Make `make check`. Pending acceptance on actual main (#1).
