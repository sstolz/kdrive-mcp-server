## Rule

Every resumed collection invocation obtains current policy authorization before publishing its items; retained state is never a grant.

## Rationale

A previous page cannot authorize disclosure after permissions or membership change. Source: [F-006 v1](../features/F-006-filtered-pagination.md).

## Examples

- **Given** a queued received candidate.
  **When** membership expires and is removed at60000ms.
  **Then** the resumed batch omits it after fresh checks.
- **Given** an authorized folder handle.
  **When** its folder disappears before continuation.
  **Then** the continuation refuses without a data call.
