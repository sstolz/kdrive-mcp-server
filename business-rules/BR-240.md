## Rule

**v1 (active until v2 acceptance)**

An append resolves only after the complete record was passed to one write call and that call wrote every byte, followed, for the file sink, by a successful `fdatasync`, and, for the stderr sink, by an error-free write callback.

**v2 (F-039 v1, #47 lot C, pending acceptance; v1 stays the active rule until then)** — The v1 write and durability conditions hold, but acknowledgement also requires completion strictly before the BR-259 v2 deadline while the sink is healthy. At or after that deadline an append rejects; a late callback, write or sync completion never acknowledges a rejected append or clears the latch.

## Rationale

An intent must be durable before the write it announces (D-16-7). Source: [F-016 v1](../features/F-016-mutation-audit-gate.md).

Pending v2: A success callback alone cannot acknowledge an append after its allowed wait has expired. Source: [F-039 v1](../features/F-039-bounded-audit-sink.md).

## Examples

- **Given** a file sink whose `fdatasync` has not completed
  **When** an append runs
  **Then** the append is still pending
- **Given** a stderr sink whose write callback has not fired
  **When** an append runs
  **Then** the append is still pending

Pending v2 examples (the accepted examples above remain historical/current as labelled):

- **Given** a stderr callback or complete file write plus sync at 4,999 ms from append
  **When** completion is processed
  **Then** the append resolves exactly once without latching. Maps to `TestBR240_v2_AC06_success_requires_completion_before_deadline`.
- **Given** the same completion at 5,000 or 6,000 ms
  **When** completion is processed even before a delayed timeout callback
  **Then** the append rejects AUDIT_UNAVAILABLE and latches, without a later success. Maps to `TestBR240_v2_AC06_success_requires_completion_before_deadline`.
- **Given** a healthy sink and an authorized rename
  **When** intent and outcome complete within their budgets
  **Then** the unchanged exact succeeded result and intent/outcome records are returned with no diagnostic. Maps to `TestBR240_v2_AC10_normal_audit_path_is_unchanged`.
