# BR-042 — External access separation

## Rule

The configured-tree authorization route denies received-share discovery and external-drive operations regardless of configured roots, known IDs or shared-access switches; only the separate F-004 route can authorize received-share operations.

The normalized evidence, matching, effect and paging details in
[F-003 v2](../features/F-003-tree-access-policy.md) and the separate route in
[F-004 v1](../features/F-004-received-share-policy.md) are jointly frozen after
fresh independent round 2 approval. The prior v1 contract remains preserved
in [history](../features/history/F-003-v1.md).

## Rationale

Preserve received-share work as a separate versioned contract.

## Examples

These are synthetic injected cases, not provider observations.

- **Given** configured drive 7 and a target on drive 8, **When** reading the target with shared switches off, **Then** the operation is denied.
- **Given** the same external target with shared switches on, **When** reading or simulating replacement through the configured-tree route, **Then** the operation is denied.
- **Given** opt-in and a complete permitted shared-root proof, **When** reading through the separate F-004 facade route, **Then** the read is permitted without adding an external configured drive.

Both examples map to the test or parameter rows named below.
