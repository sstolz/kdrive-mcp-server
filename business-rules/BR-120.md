## Rule

Content/worker errors and diagnostics serialize only declared fixed strings and never dependency messages, content, URLs or credentials.

## Rationale

Errors and worker diagnostics must not reflect confidential data. Source: [F-009 v1](../features/F-009-bounded-content-reading.md).

## Examples

- **Given** a dependency error containing CANARY_TOKEN_URL.
  **When** a read fails.
  **Then** only fixed sanitized error text is published.
- **Given** an authorized ordinary text read.
  **When** the result succeeds.
  **Then** intentional authorized text is returned.
