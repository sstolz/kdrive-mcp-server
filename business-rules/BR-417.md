# BR-417 — Admitted PDF fonts

## Rule

Only fonts selected by a kept `Tf` are read, at most 128 distinct fonts per invocation; a `Tf` naming no font of the page's Font resource refuses. Admitted: (a) `Type1`, `MMType1` or `TrueType` with a `ToUnicode` CMap, or an `/Encoding` of `WinAnsiEncoding`, `MacRomanEncoding` or `StandardEncoding`, or an encoding dictionary with optional `/Type /Encoding`, one of those as optional `/BaseEncoding` and optional `/Differences` of at most 256 codes with glyph names of at most 31 bytes, or no `/Encoding` when `/BaseFont` is one of the 14 standard fonts; (b) `Type0` with `/Encoding` `Identity-H` or `Identity-V`, one `CIDFontType0` or `CIDFontType2` descendant, and a `ToUnicode` CMap. The projected font keeps `Type`, `Subtype`, `BaseFont`, `FirstChar`, `LastChar`, `Widths` (at most 256 numbers), `Encoding`, `ToUnicode` and, for Type0, one descendant with `Type`, `Subtype`, `BaseFont`, `CIDSystemInfo`, `DW` and `W`; a `FontDescriptor` keeps `Type`, `FontName`, `Flags`, `FontBBox`, `ItalicAngle`, `Ascent`, `Descent`, `CapHeight`, `StemV` and `MissingWidth`. `FontFile`, `FontFile2`, `FontFile3` and a stream `CIDToGIDMap` are never decoded or emitted. A `ToUnicode` CMap is parsed with the bounded tokenizer: only code space ranges, `bfchar` and `bfrange` entries (the array form included) count, codes are 1 or 2 bytes, destinations are UTF-16BE of at most 64 bytes, `usecmap` refuses, a CMap has at most 65,536 entries and all admitted CMaps together cover at most 262,144 codes; it is re-emitted as a canonical CMap built from the admitted entries. Every other font refuses as `UNSUPPORTED_FORMAT`.

## Rationale

Common generators use named font resources, TrueType and Type0 subset fonts (F-034 S09–S11). PDF.js extracts text from encodings and `ToUnicode` without font programs (F-034 Context, finding 3), so programs, the largest parser surface, are never decoded; a font whose only mapping is its program refuses. Source: [F-034 v1](../features/F-034-pdf-extraction-profile.md).

## Examples

- **Given** a Type0 Identity-H font with `ToUnicode` and a `FontFile2` of random bytes
  **When** page 1 is read
  **Then** its text, and the canonical bytes hold no font program
- **Given** a Type0 font without `ToUnicode`
  **When** page 1 is read
  **Then** `UNSUPPORTED_FORMAT` before PDF.js
