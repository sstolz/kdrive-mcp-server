# BR-291 — CIMD origin list setting

## Rule

The F-002 setting `oauthAllowedClientIdOrigins` (environment `OAUTH_ALLOWED_CLIENT_ID_ORIGINS`, comma-separated like the other list settings; option `--oauth-allowed-client-id-origins`; YAML `oauthAllowedClientIdOrigins`) is an optional list, default empty, of at most 16 distinct entries of at most 1,024 characters. Each entry is `https://<host>` or `https://<host>:<port>` where `<host>` is a DNS name of at least two dot-separated labels, each label 1–63 characters of ASCII lowercase letters, digits and hyphens, not starting or ending with a hyphen (an internationalized name is written as its A-labels, `xn--…`), the last label not all digits, at most 253 characters, not `localhost`, no trailing dot; `<port>` is 1–65535 without a leading zero and other than `443`; there is no path, trailing slash, userinfo, query or fragment; and the entry is byte-identical to the WHATWG URL origin serialization of itself. Any other entry is refused with `INVALID_VALUE`. It is accepted only with transport `http`, authentication on and OAuth mode `proxy` (the BR-290 restriction); otherwise `CONFLICTING_SETTINGS`. An entry equal to the origin of `publicUrl` or of `oauthIssuer` is refused with `CONFLICTING_SETTINGS`.

## Rationale

CIMD fetches a URL named by an unauthenticated request; the operator list limits it to known client vendors and keeps CIMD off by default (D-1, D-2). Source: [F-028 v2](../features/F-028-client-id-metadata-documents.md).

## Examples

- **Given** the environment value `https://client.example.com,https://other.example.org:8443`
  **When** the configuration loads
  **Then** `oauthAllowedClientIdOrigins` is that list of two origins
- **Given** 17 entries, `https://client.example.com:443`, `https://Client.example.com` or `https://a.123`
  **When** the configuration loads
  **Then** `INVALID_VALUE` (`oauthAllowedClientIdOrigins`)
- **Given** OAuth mode `delegate`, or the entry `https://service.example` equal to the `publicUrl` origin
  **When** the configuration loads
  **Then** `CONFLICTING_SETTINGS` (`oauthAllowedClientIdOrigins`)
