# BR-384 — Waiting time is not charged to the invocation

## Rule

**v1 (active until v2 acceptance)**

An invocation's 10,000 ms deadline, its 512 dependency-call budget, its five-list budget, its output bounds and its pagination state start when it takes the slot; the time spent waiting counts toward none of them.

**v2 (F-038 v1, #47 lot B, pending acceptance; v1 stays the active rule until then)** — The v1 slot-acquisition timing and reset rules hold, with the 512 budget counting upstream dependency operations and admitted retries, excluding reads from already-open response bodies, including EOF probes (BR-070 v4). Every new invocation gets its own full operation budget and deadline; neither queue waiting nor previous invocations spend them, and no body read resets or extends the 10,000 ms invocation deadline. The five-list and output bounds remain unchanged.

## Rationale

Every invocation keeps the full bounds it has today; a queued call must not time out because of the call ahead of it. Source: [F-026 v1](../features/F-026-bounded-invocation-queue.md).

v2 aligns the slot-owned budget with BR-070 v4 while retaining queue isolation and the original deadline. Source: [F-038 v1](../features/F-038-stream-budget-and-weak-etags.md), AC-04–AC-06.

## Examples

- **Given** a call that waited 9,000 ms
  **When** its port answers 9,500 ms after it took the slot
  **Then** it succeeds
- **Given** a call that waited 9,000 ms and a port that never answers
  **When** time passes
  **Then** it returns `REQUEST_TIMEOUT` 10,000 ms after it took the slot (19,000 ms after it joined)
- **Given** a first invocation that made 512 dependency calls
  **When** the next invocation runs
  **Then** it can make 512 dependency calls and five list pages

Pending v2 examples (the preceding examples describe active v1):

- **Given** a previous invocation spent 512 operations and the next waited 9,000 ms
  **When** the next takes the slot
  **Then** it has a fresh 512-operation budget; a pending body read times out 10,000 ms after its own slot acquisition, not after queue entry. Maps to `TestBR384_v2_AC06_waiting_and_prior_operations_do_not_spend_stream_deadline`.
- **Given** fragments arrive through 9,999 ms after slot acquisition, then a read remains pending
  **When** time reaches 10,000 ms
  **Then** `REQUEST_TIMEOUT` settles the invocation with one body close and no deadline extension. Maps to `TestBR070_v4_AC04_pending_data_and_EOF_obey_deadline`.
