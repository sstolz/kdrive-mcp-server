# BR-419 — Form XObject inlining

## Rule

A `Do` whose name the resource scope's `/XObject` maps to a stream with `/Subtype /Form` (any `/Group`, `/Ref`, `/OC`, `PTEX.*` or other key ignored) is inlined: the canonical content receives `q`, then `cm` with the Form's `/Matrix` when it is present and not the identity, then the Form's projected tokens, then `Q`; a `/Matrix` that is not six finite numbers refuses as `CONTENT_UNAVAILABLE`. The Form's content is its decoded stream, decoded and tokenized once per invocation and checked with BR-416, starting outside `BT` with the caller's graphics state and font; content that ends inside `BT`/`ET` refuses as `CONTENT_UNAVAILABLE`. Its resource scope is its own `/Resources`, or, when it has none, the resource scope of the content that invoked it; its `Tf`, `gs` and `Do` names resolve in that scope, and fonts it selects take the canonical names of BR-415. The projection of a Form is built once per pair of Form and resource scope and reused for later executions with that scope, its nested executions counted again each time. An inlined Form whose projected tokens, nested Forms included, hold no text-show operator (`Tj`, `TJ`, `'`, `"`) emits nothing; one that shows text before its own first `Tf` needs a font set by its caller (BR-416). Bounds per invocation: at most 256 distinct Forms, nesting at most 8 Forms deep, and at most 4,096 Form executions in total (every `Do` of a Form counts, with or without text); a Form reached again on its own `Do` path is a cycle and refuses as `CONTENT_UNAVAILABLE`; an XObject of another subtype than `Form`, `Image` or `PS` refuses as `UNSUPPORTED_FORMAT`.

## Rationale

OCR text layers, overlays, imposition, LaTeX figures and browser output show text through Form XObjects (F-034 S21–S25). Inlining each execution into the page's canonical content keeps PDF.js on one checked stream and makes every execution count toward the static bound; the execution, nesting and distinct-Form ceilings stop exponential nesting before any output is built (F-034 D-4). Source: [F-034 v1](../features/F-034-pdf-extraction-profile.md).

## Examples

- **Given** an OCRmyPDF page whose invisible text layer is a Form
  **When** page 1 is read
  **Then** its text
- **Given** text Forms nested 8 deep
  **When** page 1 is read
  **Then** its text
- **Given** text Forms nested 9 deep
  **When** page 1 is read
  **Then** `LIMIT_EXCEEDED` before PDF.js
- **Given** a Form drawn 4,096 times
  **When** page 1 is read
  **Then** its text
- **Given** a Form drawn 4,097 times
  **When** page 1 is read
  **Then** `LIMIT_EXCEEDED` before PDF.js
- **Given** a Form cycle A→B→A
  **When** page 1 is read
  **Then** `CONTENT_UNAVAILABLE` before PDF.js
