## Rule

**v2 (F-014 v1, pending acceptance).** The runtime image keeps v1's stage settings and `--version` behaviour; run with `--http` under the BR-180 flags, `--network none` and the synthetic secret files, it prints exactly `Configuration error [PREFLIGHT_FAILED] (preflight).` and exits 1. Verified by: AC-20. ### Qualification - **BR-211** — `make kdrive-record` runs only inside the dev Compose service, reads the token and the private fixture manifest from the BR-212 directory, issues at most 200 GET requests within 600,000 ms through the production transport, and writes only sanitized recordings under `artifacts/kdrive-recordings/<run>/`; it writes nothing when the BR-213 leak check fails.

v1 below stays the accepted rule until v2 is accepted on actual main.

### v1 (accepted)

The `runtime` stage sets `ENV NODE_ENV=production`, `STOPSIGNAL SIGTERM`, `EXPOSE 3000` and `ENTRYPOINT ["node", "/app/dist/cli.js"]` with no `CMD`. Running the runtime image with `--version` prints exactly the `version` of `package.json` and exits 0. Running it with `--http`, under the BR-180 hardening flags and `--network none`, with proxy-mode settings whose URLs and identities are RFC 2606 names and whose credentials are synthetic files generated in the BR-180 tmpfs and passed only through `KDRIVE_TOKEN_FILE`, `OAUTH_UPSTREAM_CLIENT_SECRET_FILE` and `OAUTH_SIGNING_KEY_FILE`, prints `runtime is not implemented yet` and exits 1.

The image is the packaged CLI; until #6/#7 it must not pretend to serve kDrive. Source: [F-013 v1](../features/F-013-http-image.md).
