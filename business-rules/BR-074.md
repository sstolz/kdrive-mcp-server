## Rule

A continuation traversal emits each `(drive_id,id)` at most once across pages and overlapping roots.

## Rationale

Root overlap and repeated provider pages must not duplicate emitted identities. Source: [F-006 v1](../features/F-006-filtered-pagination.md).

## Examples

- **Given** 1001 children spanning five pages.
  **When** list follows cursors to terminal.
  **Then** the exact1001 identity union appears once.
- **Given** equal item IDs on owner drives8 and9.
  **When** received roots are listed.
  **Then** both owner identity pairs appear.
