## Rule

The extraction worker starts with `env: {}`, `argv: []` and `execArgv: []`. This is hygiene, not a boundary (D-13).

## Rationale

The extraction worker parses untrusted content and needs neither the environment nor arguments; this is hygiene, not a boundary (D-13). Source: [F-014 v1](../features/F-014-production-kdrive-adapter.md).

## Examples

- **Given** an extraction worker
  **When** it starts
  **Then** its `process.env` is `{}`.
- **Given** the same worker
  **When** it reads `process.argv.slice(2)` and `process.execArgv`
  **Then** both are empty.
