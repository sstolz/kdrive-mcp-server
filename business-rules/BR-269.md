# BR-269 — No adapter deadline for mutations

## Rule

A mutation request has no adapter deadline other than the gate's attempt signal (BR-249); that signal's abort destroys the socket at once and the request rejects under BR-267.

## Rationale

A slow large rename or trash must not become `uncertain` at 5,000 ms (D-17-6). Source: [F-017 v1](../features/F-017-controlled-tree-mutations.md).

## Examples

- **Given** a server that never answers
  **When** the gate attempt aborts at 30,000 ms
  **Then** the socket is destroyed and the port throws `MUTATION_UNKNOWN`
- **Given** a request still queued
  **When** its signal aborts
  **Then** the port throws `NotSent`
