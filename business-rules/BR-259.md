## Rule

**v1 (F-016 v1; superseded by v2)**

A file-sink append that has not resolved 5,000 ms of monotonic time after its write started rejects and latches the sink (BR-241); an intent append timeout then gives BR-244 and an outcome append timeout gives BR-252. The stderr sink has no append time limit (D-16-7), but on either sink an intent append that resolves more than 5,000 ms after it started gives zero attempts and an outcome record `failed` with code `AUDIT_UNAVAILABLE`, so a write is never dispatched long after its policy decision.

**v2 (F-039 v1, #47; accepted on main `8bc1780`)** — Every file or stderr append has one ordinary 5,000 ms monotonic budget from its append call, including queue wait, write and fdatasync or stderr callback. An unresolved append at or after the deadline rejects with AuditUnavailable (AUDIT_UNAVAILABLE) and latches the sink under BR-241 v2; starting I/O never resets the budget. For stdio shutdown without a run past intent, BR-070 v5 shortens non-committed appends, including denied, to the remaining shutdown deadline before run drain; it never extends an append deadline or races a committed run. Intent rejection gives BR-244 (zero attempts, failed/AUDIT_UNAVAILABLE, no outcome append); outcome rejection preserves BR-252's classification and outcome_unrecorded marker. Independently, intent acknowledgement observed by the gate at or after 5,000 ms from its append call prevents dispatch and attempts a failed/AUDIT_UNAVAILABLE outcome with zero attempts. Failure of that outcome adds the existing unrecorded marker. This replaces v1's unlimited asynchronous stderr wait and strictly-greater-than stale-intent threshold. Synchronous fd 2 writes to a file or TTY that block the event loop prevent timers from firing and remain unbounded, outside this guarantee; README/deployment guidance states this limit and that an intent without an outcome is ambiguous, with the client's mutation result as authority (a shutdown HTTP 503 does not determine whether a write occurred).

## Rationale

A stalled disk must not hold a write or dispatch it long after its decision (D-16-7). Source: [F-016 v1](../features/F-016-mutation-audit-gate.md).

v2: Reusing the accepted file allowance for all append waits bounds blocked stderr and queue congestion without weakening fail-closed classification. Source: [F-039 v1](../features/F-039-bounded-audit-sink.md).

## Examples

- **Given** a file sink whose `fdatasync` never settles
  **When** an intent append is 5,000 ms old
  **Then** it rejects, the sink latches and the run returns `AUDIT_UNAVAILABLE`
- **Given** a stderr intent callback firing at 6,000 ms
  **When** the run continues
  **Then** it makes zero attempts and records outcome `failed`/`AUDIT_UNAVAILABLE`

v2 examples (the preceding examples describe superseded v1):

- **Given** a stderr intent whose callback never fires
  **When** 4,999 then 5,000 ms pass from append
  **Then** the run is pending then failed/AUDIT_UNAVAILABLE with zero attempts and no outcome write. Maps to `TestBR259_v2_AC01_blocked_stderr_intent_fails_closed_at_5000_ms`.
- **Given** append A at 0 ms and B at 1,000 ms with A completing at 4,000 ms
  **When** B's write remains pending until 6,000 ms
  **Then** B times out at 6,000 ms, not 9,000 ms, and the sink latches. Maps to `TestBR259_v2_AC03_queue_time_is_inside_each_append_budget`.
- **Given** an acknowledged intent and a sole 200 response
  **When** its outcome callback never fires for 5,000 ms
  **Then** the result stays succeeded with status 200, attempts 1, value and outcome_unrecorded; the fixed diagnostic is offered once. Maps to `TestBR259_v2_AC04_outcome_failure_preserves_each_classification`.
- **Given** the supported Node child runtime with stderr connected to an unread pipe, a finite synthetic prefill and the real sink
  **When** one audit append stalls
  **Then** it times out/latches at the 5,000 ms budget (at most 5,500 ms observed with scheduling tolerance), reports over IPC and the parent kills the child under a 10,000 ms watchdog. Maps to `TestBR259_v2_AC15_unread_child_stderr_pipe_keeps_append_timeout_runnable`; this runtime evidence is planned, not yet measured.
- **Given** a non-committed denied append pending at stdio shutdown
  **When** the earlier shared shutdown deadline expires
  **Then** the append rejects/latches without extending that deadline or changing its denied result. Maps to `TestBR070_v5_AC13_denied_append_shares_remaining_stdio_shutdown_budget`.
