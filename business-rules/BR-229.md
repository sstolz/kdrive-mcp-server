## Rule

`.github/workflows/runner-acceptance.yml` is triggered only by `workflow_dispatch` with the optional string inputs `lan_target` and `host_target` (`<address>:<port>`), has `permissions: contents: read`, no `secrets` reference, no `env` at workflow or job level, and three jobs on the BR-225 label array, each with `timeout-minutes: 10` and the pinned checkout with `persist-credentials: false`: `probe` (`make runner-probe`, receiving the two inputs only in that step's `env`), `reset-mark` (`make runner-reset-mark`) and `reset-check` (`needs: [reset-mark]`, `make runner-reset-check`).

## Rationale

The acceptance workflow proves the isolation without any secret and only on the owner's dispatch. Source: [F-015 v1](../features/F-015-self-hosted-runner.md).
