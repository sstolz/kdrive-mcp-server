## Rule

PDF text contains only the requested ordered pages with declared separators, and a selection without extractable non-whitespace text returns an explicit no-text result without OCR.

## Rationale

Selected-page extraction must not imply OCR or silently include other pages. Source: [F-009 v1](../features/F-009-bounded-content-reading.md).

## Examples

- **Given** three text pages one/two/three.
  **When** pages[1,3] are selected.
  **Then** one and three are joined by LF/FF/LF with total_pages3.
- **Given** an image-only selected page.
  **When** text extraction runs.
  **Then** NO_EXTRACTABLE_TEXT is returned without OCR.
