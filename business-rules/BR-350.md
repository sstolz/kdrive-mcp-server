# BR-350 — Privacy scan fork notice

## Rule

Step 1 of `privacy-scan` has the `if` exactly `github.event_name == 'pull_request' && github.event.pull_request.head.repo.full_name != github.repository` and the `run` exactly the `PRIVACY_FORK_NOTICE` constant of `tests/helpers/ci-policy.ts`: `printf '%s\n' 'privacy-scan: fork pull request; deny patterns are unavailable, no scan was run.'` (exit 0). Fork PRs still fail CI through `fork-refusal` (BR-149 v2), and the merged commit is scanned on push to `main`.

## Rationale

Fork PRs get no secrets; a visible reason in the `privacy-scan` log beats a silently skipped job (D-8). Source: [F-023 v1](../features/F-023-hosted-ci-privacy-scan.md).
