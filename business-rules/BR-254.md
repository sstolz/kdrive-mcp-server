## Rule

Closing the gate refuses new runs at admission, waits for each in-flight run to append or fail its outcome (bounded by that run's total deadline plus two bounded appends, BR-259; with the stderr sink the wait also depends on stderr draining, D-16-7), then closes the sink. On shutdown the composition closes in this order: (1) the transport (HTTP `close()` per BR-169, or the stdio session), (2) the gate, (3) process exit. An HTTP request whose run is in flight at step (1) receives BR-164's 503 while the gate still completes the run and records its outcome; that 503 does not state whether the write was performed, and the audit outcome is the authority.

## Rationale

Shutdown must not lose the outcome of a dispatched write (D-16-14). Source: [F-016 v1](../features/F-016-mutation-audit-gate.md).

## Examples

- **Given** an in-flight run whose mutator settles after `close()`
  **When** `close()` resolves
  **Then** the outcome was recorded before the sink closed
- **Given** the HTTP composition with a committed run
  **When** SIGTERM arrives
  **Then** the client gets 503, the outcome is recorded, then the process exits
