## Rule

PDF extraction accepts only the bounded preflight profile and never starts PDF.js on unsupported, malformed or over-budget containers.

## Rationale

Compressed source size alone does not bound parser expansion or text production. Source: [F-009 v1](../features/F-009-bounded-content-reading.md).

## Examples

- **Given** classic xref, one Flate page stream and simple Helvetica WinAnsi.
  **When** admission runs.
  **Then** bounded canonical uncompressed bytes reach PDF.js.
- **Given** a4097-byte Flate expansion with budget4096.
  **When** preflight runs.
  **Then** LIMIT_EXCEEDED occurs before PDF.js.
