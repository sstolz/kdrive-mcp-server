## Rule

OAuth routes call the F-010 handlers as in F-010 Contract details: `query` is the query parsed as `URLSearchParams`, `form` is the body parsed as `application/x-www-form-urlencoded`, `body` is the body text, `cookie` is the `Cookie` value when present and `authorization` is the `Authorization` value when present (both as combined by BR-170). The handler's status, headers (including every `Set-Cookie` and `WWW-Authenticate`) and body are sent unchanged. A handler that throws yields the 500 of BR-170.

## Rationale

OAuth routes pass the request unchanged to the F-010 handlers and return their answer unchanged. Source: [F-012 v1](../features/F-012-authenticated-http.md), AC-15.

## Examples

- **Given** two `Cookie` headers `a=1` and `b=2`
  **When** the callback route is called
  **Then** the handler receives cookie `a=1; b=2`.
- **Given** a handler that returns two `Set-Cookie` fields
  **When** the response is written
  **Then** both arrive as separate fields.
