## Rule

Errors and diagnostics contain only declared fixed strings and never serialize content, credentials, raw headers, URLs or dependency errors.

## Rationale

Provider messages/content and caller canaries must not escape through diagnostic paths. Source: [F-007 v1](../features/F-007-bounded-downloads.md).

## Examples

- **Given** a resource exhausts branded503 reads.
  **When** its JSON-RPC failure is serialized.
  **Then** only code-32000 and READ_UNAVAILABLE appear, without error.data.
- **Given** an unbranded error has a message getter containing a token/content canary.
  **When** a tool or resource read fails.
  **Then** the getter is never called and no canary appears in errors/stderr.
