## Rule

Continuation state is bounded in age, count and retained data and fails without a partial success when those bounds cannot be maintained.

## Rationale

Long traversals and abandoned handles must not create unbounded retained state. Source: [F-006 v1](../features/F-006-filtered-pagination.md).

## Examples

- **Given** 16 inactive live traversals.
  **When** traversal17 starts.
  **Then** the least recently used inactive traversal is evicted.
- **Given** 8192 emitted identities.
  **When** another unseen identity would publish.
  **Then** LIMIT_EXCEEDED occurs without partial success.
